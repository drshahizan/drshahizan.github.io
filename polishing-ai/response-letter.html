Response to reviewers

How to structure a response letter, with model replies to the comments authors receive most often.

Principles

  • Answer every comment, in order, quoting it exactly.
  • Thank briefly, then say what you changed and where.
  • Quote the revised text so reviewers do not have to search for it.
  • Disagree politely, with evidence, and offer a partial change where you can.
  • Never promise analyses or changes you have not made.
  • Use AI to improve the tone and clarity of your replies, not to decide your position.

Draft replies with the prompt builder (task: “Draft a response to a reviewer comment”).

Letter template

Dear Dr [EDITOR NAME],

Thank you for the opportunity to revise our manuscript, "[TITLE]" (Manuscript ID: [ID]), for [JOURNAL]. We are grateful to the editor and reviewers for their constructive comments, which have helped us improve the paper.

We have addressed every comment. Below, each comment is followed by our response and a description of the changes. Page and line numbers refer to the revised manuscript, in which changes are highlighted.

The main changes are:
1. [Main change 1]
2. [Main change 2]
3. [Main change 3]

We hope the revised manuscript is now suitable for publication.

Yours sincerely,
[CORRESPONDING AUTHOR], on behalf of all authors

Model replies to common comments

Replies refer to the fictional study used in the worked example. Adapt the substance to your own study.

Reviewer: “The novelty of this study is unclear.”

Response: Thank you for raising this. We agree that the contribution was not stated clearly enough. We have revised the final paragraph of the Introduction to specify how this study differs from previous work: it is the first to model perceived risk within UTAUT for students' use of generative AI in assessed work. We have also added a paragraph on theoretical contributions to the Discussion.

Location: Page 3, lines 61–70; page 18, lines 402–415

Reviewer: “The sample size is small and the sampling method is not justified.”

Response: We appreciate this point. We have added a power analysis showing that a sample of 412 exceeds the minimum of 103 required to detect medium effects (f² = .15) with seven predictors (α = .05, power = .80). We have also explained why stratified sampling by faculty was used, and acknowledged the limits on generalisability in the Limitations section.

Location: Page 7, lines 140–152; page 20, lines 448–455

Reviewer: “The English needs substantial improvement.”

Response: Thank you. The manuscript has been thoroughly revised for language and clarity by the authors, with the assistance of an AI language tool and a final check by a colleague who is a native speaker. This is declared in the AI disclosure statement, as the journal requires.

Location: Throughout; AI disclosure on page 22

Reviewer: “Recent literature is missing.”

Response: Thank you for this suggestion. We have added eleven studies published between 2024 and 2026, including the three studies the reviewer recommended, and revised the literature review to discuss how they relate to our hypotheses.

Location: Pages 4–6; reference list

Reviewer: “The authors over-interpret their results.”

Response: We agree. We have revised the Discussion and Conclusion to avoid causal language, since the data are cross-sectional. For example, “social influence drives intention” now reads “social influence was associated with intention”.

Location: Page 16, lines 360–371; page 21, lines 470–478

Reviewer: “Please add a mediation analysis.”

Response: Thank you for this suggestion. We considered it carefully but have not added a mediation analysis, because the study was not designed to test mediation and no mediating hypotheses were specified in advance. Adding post hoc mediation would risk capitalising on chance. We have noted this as a direction for future research.

Location: Page 21, lines 480–484

Reviewer: “Was AI used to write this manuscript?”

Response: Thank you for asking. As stated in our disclosure, we used [TOOL] only to improve the language and readability of the Abstract, Introduction and Discussion. All content, analyses and references were produced and verified by the authors, who take full responsibility for the manuscript.

Location: Page 22, AI disclosure statement
