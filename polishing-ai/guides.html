Guides

Reference material to keep open while you polish. Each guide focuses on one decision you have to make about your manuscript.

  • Worked example: one abstract, five rounds

    Follow a draft abstract through structure, language, hedging, style and a final human check, with every change shown.

  • Weak vs stronger: section by section

    Side-by-side examples for the title, abstract, introduction, literature review, methods, results, discussion, limitations and conclusion.

  • Academic phrasebank and words to avoid

    Phrases for stating a gap, aim, contribution, hedging and limitations, plus overused words and plainer alternatives.

  • Publisher AI policies at a glance

    What eight major publishers allow, where they want disclosure, and their rules on images.

  • Reporting guideline finder

    Pick your study type and see which reporting guideline reviewers will expect.

  • Verify before you submit

    Six questions for every AI-assisted paragraph, an evidence-to-claim map, five review perspectives and a responsible-AI checklist.

  • AI pitfalls and real cases

    How AI output goes wrong, and published cases where it led to retractions.

  • Response to reviewers

    A response letter template and model replies to common reviewer comments.

  • Resources

    Links to ethics guidance, indexing services and verification tools.
