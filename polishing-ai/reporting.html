Reporting guideline finder

Reviewers in many fields expect a recognised reporting guideline. Choose your study type to see which one applies, then ask AI to check your methods against its checklist.

Computing and engineering studies

Most experimental computing work has no EQUATOR guideline. Follow the journal's reproducibility requirements, describe datasets, parameters and hardware, and share code and data where you can.

Prompt to use with the checklist

“Here is the [GUIDELINE] checklist and my Methods section. List each checklist item, say whether my text addresses it, and quote the sentence. Do not rewrite my text.”

Guideline links open a search on the EQUATOR Network, where you can download the latest version of each checklist.
