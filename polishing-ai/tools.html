AI tools and databases

48 tools and databases for polishing, checking and placing your manuscript. Features, pricing and access change often, so check each official website.

UTM staff and students: use your secure account

Sign in to Gemini Notebook and Google Gemini with your @utm.my account (Google Workspace for Education Plus). Your queries and data are not used to train public AI models, which makes it the safer choice for drafts and unpublished data. A personal Google account is not recommended for sensitive UTM information. HCMUT participants should use the tools their institution approves.

Indexing databases 11

Check where a journal is indexed and search the literature reviewers expect you to cite.

  • Web of Science Core CollectionInstitutional

    Search indexed literature and check citation records in WoS journals.

  • ScopusInstitutional

    Search Elsevier's abstract and citation database and check author and source profiles.

  • Google ScholarFree

    Check that a paper exists, find full text and see who cites it.

  • PubMedFree

    Search biomedical and life sciences literature.

  • IEEE XploreInstitutional

    Search engineering and computing journals and conference papers.

  • ScienceDirectInstitutional

    Read full-text articles from Elsevier journals.

  • DimensionsFreemium

    Search publications, grants and citations in one place.

  • The LensFree

    Open search across scholarly works and patents.

  • OpenAlexFree

    Open catalogue of scholarly works, authors, sources and institutions.

  • DOAJFree

    Check whether an open-access journal is legitimate and indexed.

  • MyCiteFree

    Malaysian citation index for locally published journals.

Journal metrics and quartiles 5

Confirm a journal's quartile and indexing status before you submit.

  • Journal Citation Reports (JCR)Institutional

    Check a journal's Journal Impact Factor and WoS quartile (Q1–Q4).

  • Web of Science Master Journal ListFree

    Confirm whether a journal is currently indexed in Web of Science.

  • Scopus Sources (CiteScore)Free

    Check CiteScore, SNIP and Scopus percentile for a journal.

  • SCImago Journal & Country RankFree

    Check SJR quartiles by subject category, based on Scopus data.

  • Think. Check. Submit.Free

    Checklist for spotting predatory journals.

AI chatbots and notebooks 6

Flexible tools for editing, restructuring and simulated review. Always verify facts and references.

  • ChatGPTFreemium

    Language editing, restructuring and reviewer simulation.

  • ClaudeFreemium

    Long documents, argument analysis and edits with explanations.

  • GeminiFreemium

    Language editing and Deep Research, integrated with Google Workspace.

  • Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM)Freemium

    Answers grounded only in the sources you upload; useful for checking your draft against your own references.

  • Microsoft CopilotFreemium

    Editing inside Word and Microsoft 365.

  • PerplexityFreemium

    Web answers with citations; verify every source it gives.

Academic writing and language 8

Built or tuned for academic style; best for final language checks.

  • PaperpalFreemium

    Academic language checks and submission readiness.

  • WritefullFreemium

    Academic language editing, titles and abstracts.

  • TrinkaFreemium

    Grammar for academic and technical writing.

  • GrammarlyFreemium

    Grammar, clarity and tone.

  • DeepL WriteFreemium

    Rephrasing and style suggestions that stay close to the original.

  • LanguageToolFreemium

    Multilingual grammar and style checker.

  • QuillBotFreemium

    Paraphrasing; use with caution, as it can shift meaning.

  • OverleafFreemium

    Collaborative LaTeX editor with journal templates.

Literature discovery and evidence 5

Find and understand studies quickly. No substitute for reading the original paper.

  • Semantic ScholarFree

    AI-powered academic search with TLDR summaries.

  • ElicitFreemium

    Find papers and extract data into tables.

  • ConsensusFreemium

    Evidence-based answers drawn from research papers.

  • SciteSubscription

    See whether later papers support or contrast a claim.

  • SciSpaceFreemium

    Read papers and ask questions about them.

Citation mapping 4

See how the literature connects, so you can spot missing key papers.

  • ResearchRabbitFreemium

    Explore networks of related papers and authors.

  • Connected PapersFreemium

    Visual graph of papers related to a seed paper.

  • LitmapsFreemium

    Citation maps that update as new papers appear.

  • VOSviewerFree

    Bibliometric maps from WoS or Scopus exports.

References and integrity 6

Manage citations and check them before an editor does.

  • ZoteroFree

    Free, open-source reference manager.

  • MendeleyFree

    Reference manager from Elsevier.

  • EndNoteSubscription

    Reference manager from Clarivate.

  • Crossref SearchFree

    Verify DOIs and reference metadata.

  • Retraction Watch DatabaseFree

    Check whether a cited paper has been retracted.

  • iThenticateInstitutional

    Similarity checking used by most publishers.
