Toolkit

Interactive tools for each stage of polishing. They run in your browser, so you can use them on your own draft during and after the session.

  • Prompt builder

    Choose a task, add your field and target journal, and get a complete prompt ready to paste into any AI chatbot.

  • Language and word-limit checker

    Paste a paragraph to flag AI-sounding words, filler, overclaiming, long sentences and mixed British/American spelling, and to check word limits.

  • AI disclosure generator

    Generate an AI-use statement and see where your publisher expects it to go.

  • Reference checker

    Paste your reference list to check each DOI against Crossref and catch references that do not exist or do not match.

  • Pre-submission checklist

    Tick off content, language, references, formatting, ethics and submission items before you press submit.
