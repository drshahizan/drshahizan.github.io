Hands-on activities

Six short exercises designed for an online session on Cisco Webex. Each one is linked to the relevant module.

1

Before and after

Participants polish their own abstract live, then compare the original with the final version and note what changed.

Duration
10 minutes
Format
Individual, in session
Materials
Your own abstract
Outcome
A polished abstract with a list of changes
Module
M05 Polishing section by section
2

Prompt challenge

Everyone writes a prompt for the same sample paragraph and posts the result in the Webex chat. The group votes for the clearest version that keeps the original meaning.

Duration
10 minutes
Format
Whole group via chat
Materials
A sample paragraph shared on screen
Outcome
The best prompt, added to the class prompt library
Module
M04 Prompting for academic polishing
3

Spot the fake reference

Participants receive a list of AI-generated references and identify which ones do not exist, using DOIs and databases.

Duration
10 minutes
Format
Webex breakout rooms or individual
Materials
A mix of genuine and fabricated references
Outcome
A fast technique for verifying references
Module
M08 References and citation integrity
4

Desk review simulation

Groups act as editors and make desk-review decisions on three sample abstracts under time pressure.

Duration
15 minutes
Format
Webex breakout rooms
Materials
Three sample abstracts and the journal's Aims & Scope
Outcome
A list of desk-rejection reasons identified by participants
Module
M02 What Q1/Q2 journals expect
5

Write your AI disclosure statement

Participants write a disclosure statement for their own article based on the target journal's policy.

Duration
5 minutes
Format
Individual, in session
Materials
The target journal's AI policy
Outcome
A disclosure statement ready to use
Module
M01 Ethics and responsible AI use
6

Take-home prompt templates

Participants choose and adapt one prompt template for each section of their article after the session.

Duration
Self-paced
Format
Individual, after the session
Materials
The Prompts page of this site
Outcome
A personal prompt library
Module
M12 A practical workflow
