Publisher AI policies at a glance

A summary of what eight major publishers say about AI in manuscripts. Policies change often and individual journals can be stricter, so use this to know what to look for, then confirm on the official page.

Summary checked on 5 November 2026 against publisher pages and published summaries of them.

PublisherAI as authorHelp with writingGrammar or editing onlyWhere to discloseImages
ElsevierNot allowedTo improve readability and language, with human oversightNo declaration needed for basic grammar, spelling and punctuation checksSeparate section before the references: “Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process”Not allowed to create or alter images, unless AI is part of the research method
Springer NatureNot allowed (LLMs do not meet authorship criteria)Allowed if documentedGuidance is inconsistent: one section says AI-assisted copy editing need not be declared, another says it should be. Check with the journalMethods section, or an equivalent sectionGenerally not allowed; non-generative edits to images disclosed in the caption
WileyNot allowedAs a companion to writing, not a replacementGrammar and general editing tools are outside the disclosure requirementMethods section (or a disclosure or Acknowledgements section), and declared at submissionAI must not create or alter original research data, images or results
Taylor & FrancisNot allowedAllowed, including idea generation, copyediting and translation; some journals restrict use to language improvementCovered by the general disclosure requirementA specific AI-use disclosure statement in the article, naming the tool, version, how and why it was usedAllowed for conceptual illustrations and data visualisations; not for research outputs such as diagnostic images
IEEENot stated on the guideline page; authors remain accountableAllowed with disclosureGenerally outside the policy, but disclosure is recommendedAcknowledgments section: name the system, the sections affected and the level of useAI-generated figures and images must be disclosed
MDPINot allowedAllowed with disclosureBasic language editing does not require disclosure, according to secondary summaries; confirm with the journalTool named in Acknowledgments; use described in Materials and MethodsNo specific rule found; disclose any AI-generated figure
SAGENot allowedGenerative use must be disclosed at submissionAssistive tools that improve language, grammar or structure do not need disclosureDisclosed with the submission; Methods or Acknowledgements for specific usesAllowed for representative illustrations with disclosure; not for core research data
EmeraldAuthors take full responsibilityCopy-editing your own text is allowed; drafting new content with generative AI is notCopy-editing is treated like standard spelling and grammar toolsMethods and Acknowledgements, or another appropriate sectionIllustrative figures allowed if labelled; factual or evidential images not allowed

Common ground

Every publisher here holds authors fully responsible for AI-assisted content, and none accepts an AI tool as an author. Fabricated references and AI-altered research data count as misconduct everywhere.
