Verify before you submit

No AI-assisted paragraph should bypass scholarly verification. These checks come from the session slides; each section links to the slide it is based on.

Six questions for every AI-assisted paragraph

Ask all six before generated or revised text enters the manuscript. Slide 37

  1. 1
    Is every factual claim supported?

    Check each statement of fact against your data or a source you have read.

  2. 2
    Does the cited source actually support the claim?

    Open the paper. A real reference can still be the wrong one.

  3. 3
    Is any reference fabricated?

    Check every DOI, title, journal and year with the reference checker.

  4. 4
    Has the original meaning changed?

    Compare with your draft, especially results, numbers and construct names.

  5. 5
    Is the language too certain?

    Match verbs to your design: association is not causation.

  6. 6
    Is the paragraph consistent with the study?

    Terms, numbers and conclusions must match the rest of the manuscript.

Build an evidence-to-claim map

Claim → evidence → challenge → judgement → revision. Write only what the evidence can defend. Slide 32

Manuscript claimSupporting sourcesOpposing evidenceConfidenceAction
Claim 1Strong supportLimited oppositionHighRetain
Claim 2Mixed supportRelevant oppositionModerateQualify
Claim 3Weak supportImportant oppositionLowRevise or remove

Prompt: “For each claim below, list the supporting and opposing evidence from the sources I provide, rate confidence as high, moderate or low, and recommend retain, qualify, or revise. Use only the sources given.”

Use AI to discover, not to conclude

An AI summary is not a verified conclusion. Discovery becomes credible only when the original sources are checked. Slide 42

  1. Ask an exploratory question
  2. Review the AI summary
  3. Open the cited documents
  4. Verify findings in the original sources
  5. Export suitable records
  6. Refine the search

Review the manuscript from multiple perspectives

A single review pass cannot detect every type of weakness. Run five separate reviews, each with its own role. Ready-made prompts for all five are in the prompt library under 03 Strengthen Quality (W-05 to W-09). Slide 48

  • 1. Journal-fit evaluatorIs this manuscript suitable for the target journal?
  • 2. Methodology reviewerAre the methods sound, appropriate and sufficiently described?
  • 3. Evidence and argument reviewerDo the results support the claims and advance the field?
  • 4. Academic language editorIs the language clear, precise and academically appropriate?
  • 5. Pre-submission quality auditorAre all key requirements met for a strong submission?

Responsible AI writing checklist

Ready only when every item is complete. Tick them off on the interactive pre-submission checklist. Slide 51

  1. All claims verified
  2. All references checked
  3. Confidential information protected
  4. AI-generated text critically reviewed
  5. Journal AI policy checked
  6. AI assistance disclosed where required
  7. Authors approve the final manuscript
