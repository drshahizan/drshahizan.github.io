Computer Specification Audit

Inspect a computer and evaluate whether its specification is suitable for learning and data work.

Evidence to collect

  • Processor model and core count
  • Installed RAM and current utilisation
  • Storage type, capacity and available space
  • Operating-system edition and version
  • Graphics adapter
  • Network adapter and connection type

Audit record

ItemObserved specificationEvidence sourceSuitability comment
ProcessorSystem Information
MemoryTask Manager
StorageStorage settings
GraphicsDevice Manager
NetworkNetwork settings

Privacy: Do not publish serial numbers, device IDs, account names, IP addresses or other sensitive identifiers in your evidence.
