SCST1133 · Topic 01

Technology Impact and Computing Careers

Connect computing systems with their consequences, professional responsibilities and career opportunities.

01People and Purpose
Required pathway

Every technical system changes a human environment

Useful innovation combines technical capability with accessibility, safety, privacy, sustainability and measurable public or organisational value.

Applications and system considerations

DomainExampleSystem considerations
HealthcareRemote monitoring and clinical decision support.Privacy, safety, explainability, availability and accurate data.
TransportationTraffic optimisation and connected vehicles.Real-time response, sensor reliability, cybersecurity and public safety.
ConstructionBuilding information models, drones and digital twins.Interoperability, field connectivity, lifecycle data and accountability.
Industry 4.0Automation and predictive maintenance.Operational continuity, edge processing, workforce readiness and security.
EducationLearning platforms and academic analytics.Accessibility, fairness, consent, student privacy and digital inclusion.

Career pathways

Computer and Support TechnicianInstalls, diagnoses and maintains devices and operating environments.
System AdministratorManages accounts, servers, updates, backup and service availability.
Network EngineerDesigns and operates secure, reliable communication infrastructure.
Cloud or Platform EngineerBuilds scalable services, automation and virtualised infrastructure.
Cybersecurity AnalystProtects systems, investigates threats and improves security controls.
AI EngineerDevelops and deploys machine-learning systems using suitable compute platforms.
Data EngineerBuilds reliable pipelines, storage and processing systems for data products.
Data Centre EngineerOperates compute, network, storage, power and cooling infrastructure.

Professional reflection

  • Which system layer interests you most: devices, networks, platforms, security, AI or data?
  • Which technical and human skills are required for that role?
  • How could a poorly designed system harm users or the environment?
  • What evidence could demonstrate that the system creates real value?
