Networks, Internet and Cloud Services

01Introduction to Computer and System
Connectivity

Modern systems rarely operate alone

Networks connect clients, servers and shared resources across local and global environments.

Essential terms

TermMeaning
ClientA device or application requesting a service.
ServerA system providing data or functionality to clients.
NetworkConnected devices that exchange information and resources.
InternetA global system of interconnected networks.
Cloud serviceRemote computing or storage accessed through a network.
LatencyThe delay experienced while data travels and a response is produced.
