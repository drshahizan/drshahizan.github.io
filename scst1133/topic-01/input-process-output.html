01Introduction to Computer and System
Operational model

Follow information through the system

The IPO model becomes more useful when storage and feedback are included.

Course-registration example

StageExample
InputA student selects a course.
ProcessingThe system checks prerequisites and available capacity.
StorageThe registration record is saved.
OutputAn updated timetable or error message is displayed.
FeedbackThe student confirms the result or corrects the selection.

Think in relationships

Each stage depends on the others. Incorrect input, unavailable storage or delayed feedback can prevent the entire service from meeting its purpose.
