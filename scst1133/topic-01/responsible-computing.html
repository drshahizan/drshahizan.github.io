SCST1133 · Topic 01

Responsible Computing

Protect users, information and resources while considering accessibility and environmental impact.

01Introduction to Computer and System
Safety and responsibility

Good computing is safe, inclusive and sustainable

Responsible practice applies throughout the system lifecycle, not only after a problem occurs.

Account security

Use strong passwords, multifactor authentication and software updates.

Privacy

Collect, store and share personal information carefully.

Backup

Keep recoverable copies of important files.

Accessibility

Design information and interfaces for diverse users.

Sustainability

Reduce unnecessary energy use and electronic waste.

Shared resources

Follow acceptable-use rules and avoid disrupting other users.
