InterfaceTypical purposeImportant consideration
USB-A / USB-CPeripherals, data and storageConnector shape does not guarantee identical speed.
ThunderboltHigh-speed data, displays and dockingRequires compatible devices and cables.
HDMI / DisplayPortDigital display and audioVersion affects resolution and refresh rate.
EthernetWired networkingOften offers stable, low-latency connectivity.
3.5 mm audioHeadphones, speakers or microphonesPort type and headset wiring must be compatible.
