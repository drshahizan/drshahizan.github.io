Six-question analysis framework

  • What problem does the system solve?
  • Which hardware components are essential?
  • How does data move through the system?
  • Where are the main bottlenecks and failure risks?
  • How are power, cooling, security and reliability managed?
  • What improvement would deliver the greatest benefit?
