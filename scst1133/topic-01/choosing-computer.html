Choosing a Computer for a Purpose

Prioritise specifications according to workload instead of choosing the largest number in every category.

Decision process

  1. Define the principal workload and software requirements.
  2. Identify the likely bottleneck: CPU, RAM, storage, GPU, network or display.
  3. Set a realistic budget and consider future upgrades.
  4. Evaluate portability, energy, warranty and repairability.
  5. Justify the final choice using evidence rather than marketing labels.

Mini challenge: Recommend a computer for a first-year data engineering student and explain three priorities and one acceptable compromise.
