Who is submitting, and what?

Any member of the group may submit. Your matric number is checked against the group you choose.

What you can submit here

  • Topic 3 · Case-study task: your assigned case study and the link to your completed task.
  • Assignment 1 · Talk 1: GitHub and LinkedIn links. The PDF report is uploaded on UTM e-Learning.
  • Video · Case study: register your case study first. Each group must use a different one.
  • Video · YouTube link: after your case study is registered.
Before the deadline
You may submit again to replace your group's earlier submission. After the deadline, submission closes automatically.

Take a screenshot or print your receipt as proof of submission.
