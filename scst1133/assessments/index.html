Passing requirements

Students must pass both Continuous Assessment and Final Assessment and achieve the minimum passing mark required by UTM regulations.

Important
Continuous Assessment minimum: 30% of continuous marks.
Final Assessment minimum: 20% of final marks.

Academic integrity

Original work, correct acknowledgement of sources and responsible use of digital and AI tools are required. Plagiarism and unauthorised copying are not permitted.
