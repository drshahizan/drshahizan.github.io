How to submit on the website

  1. Open the submission page and enter your matric number.
  2. Choose your group (for example S03-G1) and the assignment.
  3. Fill in the links or details, then select Submit.
  4. Keep the reference number on your receipt (screenshot or print it) as proof of submission.

Submission rules

  • Any member of the group may submit for the group.
  • You may submit again before the deadline; the newest submission replaces the earlier one.
  • Register your case study first. Once the video is submitted, the case study cannot be changed.
  • All links must open without signing in: a public GitHub repository and a Public or Unlisted YouTube video.
  • If you have a problem, contact the lecturer before the deadline.
