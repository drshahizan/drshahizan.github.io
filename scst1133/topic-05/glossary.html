TermMeaning in Topic 05
Raw dataOriginal records that have not yet been prepared for a particular use.
InformationData that has been organised and given context or meaning.
Data engineeringThe practice of collecting, organising, preparing and delivering dependable data.
Data ecosystemThe connected environment of people, data, processes, responsibilities and technology.
Data producerA person, activity, device or application that creates data.
Data ownerThe party accountable for access, quality and appropriate use.
Data userA person or application that uses prepared information.
Data qualityThe degree to which data is accurate, complete, consistent and timely.
Data governanceRules, responsibilities and controls governing organisational data.
Data pipelineA connected sequence of processes that moves and prepares data.
