Step 05 · Collaborate

People in the Data Ecosystem

Data engineering is collaborative work that connects technical specialists, organisational owners and information users.

05People
Roles

Who does what?

Each role has a different focus, but dependable data requires them to work together.

RolePrimary responsibility
Data EngineerPrepares and delivers reliable data for organisational use.
Data AnalystExamines data and communicates findings.
Data ScientistExplores patterns and develops predictive solutions.
Database AdministratorManages database availability, performance and access.
Software EngineerDevelops applications that generate or use data.
Data OwnerDetermines responsibility, access and appropriate use.
Business UserUses information to perform work and support decisions.

A useful analogy

  • The data engineer prepares dependable routes for information.
  • The data analyst uses available information to answer questions.
  • The data scientist uses it to explore patterns and possible outcomes.
  • The software engineer builds applications that produce or consume it.
  • The data owner establishes how it should be managed and used.

Distinguishing three commonly confused roles

RoleMain focusTypical output
Data EngineerReliability, preparation and availabilityTrusted datasets and data pipelines
Data AnalystExplaining existing patterns and performanceReports, dashboards and findings
Data ScientistPrediction, experimentation and modellingModels, forecasts and evaluated hypotheses
Inside an organisation

Which direction does a data engineer face?

External-facing

Serves users of public applications—social media, e-commerce or IoT devices. Builds systems that collect and process transaction and event data, often feeding results back into the application. Must handle many simultaneous users and stricter security.

Internal-facing

Serves the business and internal stakeholders—pipelines and warehouses for reports, dashboards, business processes, data science and ML. In practice, many engineers do both.

Working with business leadership

Data engineers increasingly work with senior leaders who shape data strategy, including the chief executive officer, chief information officer, chief technology officer and chief data officer, as well as project managers and product managers who set priorities and deadlines.
