Questions for every case

  1. Who generates the data?
  2. Who owns or is responsible for it?
  3. Who needs to use it?
  4. What organisational value does it create?
  5. What could happen if it is incorrect, late or unavailable?
  6. What privacy or access concerns should be considered?

A consistent framework for analysing every case

1. Organisational needWhat problem or service requires data?
2. Data producersWho or what creates the records?
3. Data ownerWho is accountable for the information?
4. Engineering workHow is the data collected, checked and prepared?
5. Data usersWho uses the prepared information?
6. Value and responsibilityWhat benefit and risk should be considered?
