Step 08 · Apply

Week 1 Learning Activities

Use familiar services and simple scenarios to explore the people, purpose and movement of data.

08Activity
Class activities

Connect the concepts to everyday life

Complete the activities individually and then compare your ideas with your group.

Activity 1 · Data Around Me

Select three digital services

Choose services you used during the previous 24 hours, such as UTM Smart, Grab, Shopee or online banking.

Identify the data

List examples of information generated through each service.

Identify the people

State who produces, manages and uses the information.

Explain the value

Describe why the information is useful and what may happen if it is incorrect.

Activity 2 · Identify the stakeholders

Scenario: A university wants to monitor student attendance and identify students who may require academic support.

  • Who produces the attendance data?
  • Who owns or is responsible for it?
  • Who prepares it for use?
  • Who should be allowed to see it?
  • Who should not have access?
  • What decisions could it support?

Activity 3 · Follow the data journey

Select one scenario: course registration, food delivery, hospital appointment, library borrowing or online banking. Explain how the data is generated, collected, organised, checked, made available and used. No technical tools are required.

Activity 4 · Good data or problematic data?
