Step 12 · Analyse

Mapping Malaysia’s Open Data Ecosystem

Investigate a real Malaysian dataset and explain the ecosystem that makes it useful.

12Case Task
Group activity · 30–40 minutes

Explore data.gov.my

Work in a group of three or four. Select one dataset from Malaysia’s official Open Data Catalogue and analyse it conceptually.

Complete the investigation

Select a dataset

Visit the catalogue and choose a dataset that your group can explain clearly.

Identify the ecosystem

Record the producing agency, data owner, possible users and public purpose.

Assess its usefulness

Explain how the information could support a service, decision, research question or public understanding.

Consider responsibility

Identify one quality concern, one privacy or ethical concern and the effect of outdated or incorrect data.

Present your findings

Deliver a three-minute explanation and recommend one improvement.

Presentation criteria

  • The dataset and responsible agency are correctly identified.
  • The data producers and users are clearly distinguished.
  • The public or organisational value is explained.
  • Quality and privacy concerns are relevant.
  • The improvement is realistic and supported by reasoning.
✓

No programming is required. The purpose is to understand the ecosystem, stakeholders, responsibility and value surrounding a real Malaysian dataset.
