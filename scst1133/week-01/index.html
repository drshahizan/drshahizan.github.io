SCST1133 · Guided Learning Module

Introduction to the Data Engineering Ecosystem

Discover how people, data and organisational needs work together to turn raw information into something useful and trustworthy.

01Week
Module overview

Seeing the whole data ecosystem

This week provides a conceptual foundation. The focus is on purpose, people and relationships rather than specific technologies.

Learning outcomes

  • Define data engineering in simple terms.
  • Explain why reliable and accessible data matters to organisations.
  • Identify the people and broad elements within a data engineering ecosystem.
  • Differentiate data engineering from related computing disciplines.
  • Describe the general journey of data from generation to use.
  • Recognise data engineering in familiar digital services.
Understand

Meaning and purpose

Learn what data engineering is and why raw data needs to be organised before it becomes useful.

Connect

The ecosystem

See how data producers, organisational processes, responsible people and data users are connected.

Recognise

People and roles

Understand how data engineers collaborate with analysts, scientists, administrators and business users.

Apply

Everyday examples

Trace data through familiar services such as course registration, online banking and food delivery.

✓

Scope for Week 1: Focus on the big picture. Technical platforms, detailed architectures and implementation methods will be introduced in later weeks.
