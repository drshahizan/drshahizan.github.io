The UTM Campus Parking Crisis

Investigate a real campus problem, design with users and identify the data a smart solution needs.

17Case
Running case

Scenario: before you design an app, understand the problem

UTM is facing serious student complaints about campus parking. Management has appointed your team as systems analysts to investigate the current process and propose a Smart Parking Management System.

What students report

  • Spending 30 to 45 minutes searching for parking and arriving late to class
  • Permits are difficult to renew online
  • Enforcement appears inconsistent
!

Your brief: Do not jump straight into designing an app. First understand the people affected, define the underlying problems, explore possible solutions, build a prototype and test it with users.

Task 1 · Technique Selection Matrix (Part A)

For each investigation goal, recommend the most suitable information-gathering technique and justify your choice.

#Investigation goalGroups
1Understand high-level policy constraints and budget limits from university management1–2
2Discover the daily frustrations, parking habits and pain points of student drivers3–4
3Verify actual gate traffic, peak congestion hours and car-park capacity without user bias5–6
4Review existing paper permit forms and manual fine-payment records7–8
5Rapidly collect satisfaction statistics from more than 2,000 students9–10

Task 2 · Apply design thinking (Part B)

Describe what your team would do in each of the five modes for the Smart Parking Management System:

ModeGuiding question
EmpathiseWhom will you engage, and how? What will you observe?
DefineWrite a persona and a POV statement.
IdeateList at least eight ideas, then choose two or three.
PrototypeWhat will you build first, and with what tool?
TestWhom will you test with, and what will you measure?

Task 3 · Identify the data (Part C)

List three important data items the Smart Parking Management System would use. For each, state its type (structured, semi-structured or unstructured), its likely source system (Step 08) and whether it needs batch or real-time processing.
