Batch and Real-Time Processing

Process data later in groups, or immediately as it happens?

15Processing
Data landscape

Two ways to process data

The choice between batch and real-time processing depends on how quickly users need results.

Compared

Batch

Collect, then process together

Data is collected over a period and processed later, all at once.

  • Monthly payroll
  • End-of-semester grade reports
  • Nightly summary of parking-gate entries
Real-time

Process immediately

Also called online processing, because it happens during the transaction.

  • ATM withdrawals
  • Online seat booking
  • A display showing free parking bays now

Which should you choose?

QuestionLeans to batchLeans to real-time
How fresh must results be?Hours or days are acceptableSeconds matter
How much data per run?Large volumes at onceSmall, continuous events
Cost and simplicity?Usually simpler and cheaperMore complex to build and run
