Empathise and Define

Understand people deeply, then turn what you learn into a clear, human problem statement.

10Empathise · Define
Design

From observations to a point of view

Empathy is the foundation of human-centred design. The Define mode turns scattered findings into a focused challenge.

Empathise: three activities

Observe

Watch users and their behaviour in the context of their lives.

Engage

Talk with users through planned interviews and short, informal conversations.

Immerse

Experience what users experience yourself.

Empathy map

Place the user in the centre and record what they say, think, do and feel. Contradictions between quadrants—for example, a student who says parking is fine but does arrive late every day—often reveal the most useful insights.

Define: persona, problem and goal

A persona is a realistic description of a typical user, based on research. Combine it with the problem and the user’s goal in a point-of-view (POV) statement:

[User] needs a way to [user’s need] because [insight].

Example: A first-year commuter student needs a way to know where free parking is before arriving, because searching for 30–45 minutes makes her late for 8 a.m. classes.

A good POV is specific to a user, grounded in evidence and leaves room for many solutions.

Source: Hasso Plattner Institute of Design at Stanford (d.school). Design Thinking Bootleg; lecture slides.
