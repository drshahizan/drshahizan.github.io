Class activities

Practise each part

Complete the activities in class and tick the related items in the evidence tracker on the Overview page.

Activity 1 · Build an interview Part A · Pairs · 20 min

Write eight questions to interview a student about campus parking. Include open-ended, closed, bipolar and probing questions. Arrange them as a funnel, then rearrange as a pyramid. Which order would work better for a frustrated student? Why?

Activity 2 · Fix the questionnaire Part A · Individual · 10 min

Rewrite each poor question so it follows the language checklist:

  1. “Don’t you agree that the useless parking system should be replaced?”
  2. “How do you feel about the parking, permits, enforcement and shuttle bus?”
  3. “Rate the efficacy of the extant vehicular access control paradigm.”

Activity 3 · Sample it Part A · Individual · 10 min

Management wants to estimate the proportion of parking summonses issued in error. They expect about 10%, accept an interval estimate of 0.03 and want 95% confidence. Use the calculator to find the sample size, then choose a sampling type and justify it.

Activity 4 · Spot the sources Part A · Groups · 15 min

Pick one app you use daily. Identify one example of each source system from Step 08—file, API, application database, log and stream. Which ones could you not observe directly?

Activity 5 · Empathy map in ten minutes Part B · Pairs · 15 min

Interview your partner for five minutes about getting to campus. Complete an empathy map using the template on Empathise and Define, then write one POV statement.

Activity 6 · Brainstorm sprint Part B · Groups · 15 min

Using your POV statement, generate at least 20 ideas in eight minutes while following all eight brainstorming rules. Vote for one “most likely to delight”, one “most rational” and one “most unexpected” idea.

Activity 7 · Data scavenger hunt Part C · Groups · 15 min

Find two examples each of structured, semi-structured and unstructured data on your phone. For one structured example, identify its records, fields and key field.
