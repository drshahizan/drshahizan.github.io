Part B · Step 11 · Design

Ideate

Go wide first. Quantity of ideas leads to quality of ideas.

11Ideate
Design

Explore the widest solution space

Ideation is about generating many different ideas before choosing any. It moves from the problem you defined to possible solutions you can prototype.

Brainstorming rules

RuleWhy it helps
One conversation at a timeEvery idea is heard
Go for quantityMore ideas increase the chance of a great one
HeadlineCapture each idea in a short phrase
Build on the ideas of othersSay “yes, and…” instead of “no, but…”
Encourage wild ideasUnusual ideas often lead to practical breakthroughs
Be visualSketch ideas on sticky notes
Stay on topicKeep the POV statement visible
Defer judgementNo blocking or criticising during the session

From many ideas to a few

After brainstorming, group similar ideas and vote. Choose a mix: the most likely to delight, the most rational and the most unexpected. Carry two or three forward to prototyping.
