Part B · Step 09 · Design

What Is Design Thinking?

A human-centred way to solve problems: understand people first, then build and test ideas quickly.

09Design thinking
Design

People first, technology second

Data solutions fail when they solve the wrong problem. Design thinking keeps the people who will use a system at the centre of every decision.

ModeCore questionTypical output
EmpathiseWho are the users and what do they experience?Interview notes, observations, empathy map
DefineWhat is the real problem worth solving?Persona and point-of-view (POV) statement
IdeateWhat are many possible solutions?A wide range of ideas, then a shortlist
PrototypeHow can we make an idea tangible quickly?Sketches, paper or digital mock-ups
TestWhat do users think, and what should change?Feedback and an improved prototype

Three lenses for a good solution

Desirable

Do people want it? (human)

Feasible

Can it be built? (technology)

Viable

Can it be sustained? (organisation and information)

Impact comes where all three overlap.

Design thinking beside familiar processes

The three processes share a pattern: understand the problem, explore options, build something and evaluate it. Design thinking puts the most emphasis on understanding people.
