SCST1133 · Topic 04 · CLO1

Data Collection, Design Thinking and the Data Landscape

Learn how to gather information from people and systems, design solutions around real user needs and recognise the many forms that data takes.

04Collect · Design · Understand
Module overview

Before you build, find out

Topic 03 introduced the data engineering lifecycle. Topic 04 zooms into its first stage—where data comes from—and the human side of understanding a problem before designing a data solution.

Learning outcomes

By the end of this topic, students should be able to:

  • Compare interactive and unobtrusive methods of gathering information.
  • Construct interview questions and questionnaires, and arrange them appropriately.
  • Determine a suitable sampling approach and calculate a sample size.
  • Apply investigation and structured observation (STROBE) to understand an organisation.
  • Identify the main source systems that generate data for data engineering.
  • Apply the five modes of design thinking to a real problem.
  • Classify data as structured, semi-structured or unstructured, and explain how data is organised and processed.
