Part A · Step 06 · Unobtrusive

Investigation

Documents, forms and records reveal how an organisation really works—and where its data comes from.

06Investigation
Unobtrusive method

Read the hard data

Investigation is the discovery and analysis of existing data. Analysts examine both quantitative and qualitative documents.

Quantitative documents

DocumentWhat to look for
Reports for decision-makingWhich figures managers actually rely on
Performance reportsGaps between actual and target performance
RecordsErrors in amounts and totals; number and type of transactions; tasks a computer could simplify; better form design
Data capture formsEvery form in use, its type, intended distribution and who actually receives it
E-commerce and other transactionsWhat is captured automatically, and when

Questions to ask about forms

  • Is the form filled out completely?
  • Are there forms that are never used?
  • Do all copies reach the right people or files?
  • Can people who need online forms actually access them?
  • If paper and web versions exist, which has the higher completion rate?
  • Are unofficial (“bootleg”) forms used regularly?

Qualitative documents

Emails, memos, notice-board posters, websites, manuals and policy handbooks reveal the organisation’s culture: its guiding metaphors, insider versus outsider attitudes, what is seen as good or bad, the graphics and icons it uses, and even its sense of humour.
