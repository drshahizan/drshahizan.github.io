Key terms in one place

Search for a term or a word in its meaning.

TermMeaning
Fact-findingCollecting information about system problems, opportunities, requirements and priorities.
Interactive methodGathering information through direct interaction with people, such as interviews, JAD and questionnaires.
Unobtrusive methodGathering information without direct interaction, such as sampling, document investigation and observation.
Open-ended questionA question that lets the respondent answer freely and at length.
Closed questionA question with a limited set of possible answers.
Bipolar questionA closed question answered yes/no or agree/disagree.
Probing questionA follow-up question that draws out more detail or clarifies an answer.
Pyramid structureInterview questions ordered from specific and closed to general and open.
Funnel structureInterview questions ordered from general and open to specific and closed.
Diamond structureInterview questions ordered closed → open → closed.
Joint Application Design (JAD)A group session in which users and analysts analyse requirements and design together.
Nominal scaleA scale that classifies items; the weakest form of measurement.
Interval scaleA scale with equal intervals but no absolute zero.
ValidityThe degree to which a question measures what it is intended to measure.
ReliabilityConsistency of results when a questionnaire is repeated under the same conditions.
SamplingSelecting a representative subset of people, documents or events.
STROBESTRuctured OBservation of the Environment: observing a decision maker’s surroundings.
Source systemA system that generates data, such as an application database, API, log or stream.
APIApplication programming interface; a standard way for systems to exchange data.
OLTPOnline transaction processing; databases that handle many small reads and writes for applications.
LogA timestamped record of events that occur in a system.
Design thinkingA human-centred problem-solving process: empathise, define, ideate, prototype and test.
Empathy mapA tool that records what a user says, thinks, does and feels.
PersonaA realistic profile of a typical user based on research.
POV statementA point-of-view statement combining user, need and insight.
PrototypeA quick, tangible version of an idea that users can interact with.
Structured dataData with a fixed schema in rows and columns.
Semi-structured dataData with tags or keys but no rigid table, such as JSON or XML.
Unstructured dataData with no predefined model, such as text, images, audio and video.
Key field (primary key)A field that uniquely identifies each record.
Batch processingCollecting data over time and processing it together later.
Real-time processingProcessing data immediately as each transaction occurs.
DatabaseAn integrated collection of logically related data that can be accessed, updated and shared.
