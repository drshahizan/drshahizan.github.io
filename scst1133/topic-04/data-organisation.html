Data landscape

The data hierarchy

Structured data is organised in levels. Each level groups the one below it.

LevelDefinitionExample
CharacterA single letter, number or symbolA
FieldA group of related characters representing one attributeStudent name: Aisyah
RecordA collection of related fields about one entityOne student’s matric number, name and programme
Table (file)A collection of related recordsAll students
DatabaseA collection of integrated, related tablesStudents, courses and enrolments together

Key fields

A key field, or primary key, uniquely identifies each record. Common examples are student matric numbers, employee numbers, part numbers, inventory numbers and national identification numbers.

Without a unique key, you cannot reliably tell two records apart—for example, two students named Ahmad in the same course.
