Individual reflection

What have you learned?

Use the concept map and checklist to review Topic 04.

Exit checklist

  • I can compare interactive and unobtrusive methods and choose between them.
  • I can write open, closed and probing questions and arrange them as a pyramid, funnel or diamond.
  • I can calculate a sample size and choose a sampling type.
  • I can use STROBE to compare observation with interview findings.
  • I can name five kinds of source system that generate data.
  • I can describe and apply the five modes of design thinking.
  • I can classify data as structured, semi-structured or unstructured.
  • I can explain the data hierarchy, key fields, batch versus real-time processing and why databases matter.

Reflection prompts

  1. Which information-gathering method would you feel most confident using, and which least? Why?
  2. How did design thinking change the solution your group imagined for campus parking?
  3. Why does a data engineer need to understand both people and source systems?

Recommended length: 200–300 words, added to your UTM ePortfolio.
