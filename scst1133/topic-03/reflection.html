Individual reflection

What have you learned?

Use the concept map and checklist to confirm that you can explain the discipline in your own words.

Exit checklist

  • I can define data engineering and a data engineer in my own words.
  • I can describe the four eras and give one milestone from each.
  • I can explain why data engineering sits upstream of data science.
  • I can name the five lifecycle stages in order and explain why storage is a foundation.
  • I can name the six undercurrents and give an example of each.
  • I can explain the balancing act and what a data engineer typically does not do.
  • I can explain why SQL and Python matter.
  • I can describe two trends shaping the future of the field.

Reflection prompts

  1. Which idea in this topic changed how you think about data?
  2. Which of the six undercurrents do you feel least confident about? What will you do about it?
  3. Which of the four eras would you most like to have worked in, and why?
  4. How does the computer-system view from Topic 01 help you understand the data engineering lifecycle?

Recommended length: 200–300 words, added to your UTM ePortfolio.
