Authors · Core

Fundamentals of Data Engineering — Joe Reis and Matt Housley

The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck (about 35 minutes, recorded around the book’s release). The authors explain why they wrote the book, how they define data engineering and why they focus on the lifecycle instead of specific tools.

Best for: Steps 01, 04 and 05.

Authors · Future

Fundamentals of Data Engineering — three years on

GOTO Book Club (2025). The authors revisit the book, discuss how AI tools are changing data engineering and explain why foundational knowledge still matters.

Best for: Step 08.

Response questions

  1. How do the speakers define data engineering?
  2. Which era of the field do they describe from personal experience?
  3. Why do they emphasise the lifecycle instead of tools?
  4. What do they predict about the future, and do you agree?
