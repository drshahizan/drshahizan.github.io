The Six Undercurrents

Beneath every stage run six critical ideas. No part of the lifecycle works well without them.

05Undercurrents
Big idea

What runs beneath the lifecycle

Undercurrents cut across every stage. They are also the higher-value skills that increasingly define a data engineer’s work.

UndercurrentIn one sentenceCourse link
SecurityProtect data and systems by giving people and processes only the access they need.Topic 13
Data managementTreat data as an asset: govern it, describe it with metadata, ensure its quality and track its lineage.Topics 12 and 13
DataOpsApply Agile and DevOps thinking to data: automation, monitoring and fast response to incidents.Topic 09
Data architectureDesign data systems that meet today’s needs and can change tomorrow.Topic 09
OrchestrationCoordinate and schedule many jobs so they run in the right order and recover from failure.Topic 09
Software engineeringWrite, test, version and maintain the code that runs data systems.Topics 02 and 09

Why “undercurrents”?

Like currents beneath the surface of the sea, these ideas are not always visible, yet they shape where everything on top moves. A pipeline that ingests and transforms data perfectly can still fail if it is insecure, undocumented or impossible to monitor.
