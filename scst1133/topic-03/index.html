Introduction to Data Engineering

Understand data engineering as a discipline: what it is, where it came from, what makes it different and where it is heading.

03Introduction to Data Engineering
Module overview

Meet the discipline before the tools

Topic 01 showed how a computer works as a system. Topic 03 looks at data as a system and introduces the discipline that keeps it moving.

Learning outcomes

By the end of this topic, students should be able to:

  • Explain the meaning of data engineering and the role of a data engineer.
  • Trace the evolution of data engineering across four eras.
  • Distinguish data engineering from data science using the Data Science Hierarchy of Needs.
  • Identify the five stages of the data engineering lifecycle and its six undercurrents.
  • Describe the balancing act, core skills and primary languages of a data engineer.
  • Discuss trends that are shaping the future of data engineering.
