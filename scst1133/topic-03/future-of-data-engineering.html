The Future of Data Engineering

Tools will change quickly. The lifecycle and its fundamentals will remain.

08Future
Trends

Where the field is heading

Nobody can predict the future precisely, but the past and present show clear directions of travel.

TrendWhat it means
The lifecycle is not going awayAs organisations build data foundations before AI, the need for data engineering grows.
Simpler, easier toolsManaged services and ready-made connectors lower the barrier to entry; data engineering is no longer only for large companies.
A cloud-scale “data operating system”Standard interfaces let data services work together, much as an operating system coordinates a computer’s resources (recall Topic 01).
“Enterprisey” data engineeringData management, operations and governance become everyday work for organisations of all sizes.
Titles and responsibilities will morphBoundaries between data engineering, software engineering and machine-learning engineering become fuzzier.
From the modern data stack to the live data stackStreaming pipelines and real-time analytical databases bring live data to more organisations.
Fusion of data and applicationsApplications, analytics and machine learning become tightly connected, with fast feedback between them.
Dark matter data and spreadsheetsThe spreadsheet is the most widely used data platform, with an estimated 700 million to 2 billion users. Much analysis never leaves it, so new tools may combine spreadsheet interactivity with the power of cloud analytical databases.
Update

Since the book: data engineering in the age of generative AI

Fundamentals of Data Engineering was published in 2022. Since then, generative AI has made reliable, well-governed data even more important—models are only as good as the data used to train, tune and ground them. AI assistants are also beginning to help engineers write and debug pipeline code.

In a 2025 GOTO Book Club conversation, the authors reflect that foundational knowledge matters more, not less, when AI tools are widely available. See Videos and Podcasts.
