Step 01 · Define

What Is Data Engineering?

Many experts define the field differently. Their common thread gives us a working definition.

01Definition
Core concept

Many definitions, one common thread

Data engineering has existed in some form for as long as organisations have analysed and reported on data. It came into sharp focus alongside the rise of data science in the 2010s.

How experts describe the field

PerspectiveEmphasisIn plain words
Infrastructure viewInterfaces and mechanisms for the flow of informationEngineers keep data available and usable for others.
Technology view (Jesse Anderson)SQL-focused versus big-data-focused workSome teams work mainly in relational databases; others in distributed frameworks.

Short view (Lewis Gavin)Movement, manipulation and management of dataMoving data, changing its shape and looking after it.

A working definition

Reis and Housley define data engineering as the development, implementation and maintenance of systems and processes that take in raw data and produce high-quality, consistent information to support downstream uses such as analysis and machine learning.

A data engineer manages the data engineering lifecycle—from getting data out of source systems to serving it for use.

The definition also describes data engineering as the intersection of security, data management, DataOps, data architecture, orchestration and software engineering. You will meet these six undercurrents on Step 05.

Three verbs to remember

Get

Bring data out of the systems that create it.

Store

Keep it safely and in a form that can be reached again.

Prepare

Shape it into consistent, trustworthy information that others can use.

?

Think about it: Why might four experienced professionals define the same field so differently? What does that suggest about how young the discipline is?

Source: Reis, J. & Housley, M. (2022). Fundamentals of Data Engineering. O’Reilly Media, Chapter 1, pp. 3–5.
