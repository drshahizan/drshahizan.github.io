The Lifecycle at a Glance

Five stages turn raw data into a useful product. This is the map for the rest of the course.

04Lifecycle
Big idea

Five stages from source to use

The data engineering lifecycle shifts attention away from individual technologies and toward the data itself and the goals it must serve.

StageKey questionStudied in depth
1 · GenerationWhere does the data come from? (applications, devices, files, APIs, logs)Topic 04
2 · StorageWhere and how is the data kept as it flows?Topic 12
3 · IngestionHow is data moved from source systems into our systems?Topic 10
4 · TransformationHow is data cleaned, combined and reshaped to be useful?Topics 09 and 12
5 · ServingHow is data delivered for analytics, machine learning and applications?Topics 09 and 12

Not always a neat line

Storage happens throughout the lifecycle, which is why it is drawn as a foundation. The middle stages—storage, ingestion and transformation—often overlap, repeat or occur in a different order in real systems.

Data lifecycle versus data engineering lifecycle

The full data lifecycle covers data across its entire lifespan. The data engineering lifecycle is the subset of stages that a data engineer controls.

Topic 05 follows the broader organisational journey of data; Topic 09 builds the data engineering lifecycle in detail.
