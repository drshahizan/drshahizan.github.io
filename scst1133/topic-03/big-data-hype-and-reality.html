Big Data: Hype and Reality

A buzzword became a victim of its own success. What can we learn from it?

E2Big data
Extension

The rise and fall of a buzzword

Data is moving faster and growing larger than ever, yet the term “big data” has largely disappeared. Understanding why builds healthy scepticism toward technology hype.

A short story in four parts

Excitement

Hadoop and related tools gave every organisation access to the technology of the largest tech companies.

Hype

From the early 2000s to the mid-2010s, marketing and fear of missing out spread. Some companies built Hadoop clusters to process only a few gigabytes.

Cost

Running these platforms needed whole teams of engineers, costing millions each year, who spent more time maintaining tools than delivering value.

Simplification

Cloud providers and open-source projects hid the complexity. Large-scale processing became ordinary, and big data engineers became simply data engineers.

Lessons for future engineers

  • Choose tools for the size of the actual problem, not the most fashionable one.
  • Ask what value a technology delivers, not only what it can do.
  • Expect today’s buzzwords to follow a similar curve.

Discussion

Which current technology term do you think is at the peak of its hype? Use the four-part story above to predict what might happen to it.

Source: Reis, J. & Housley, M. (2022). Fundamentals of Data Engineering. O’Reilly Media, Chapter 1, pp. 7–9.
