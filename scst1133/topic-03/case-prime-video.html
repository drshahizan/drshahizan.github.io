Prime Video: Back to a Monolith

A team reduced infrastructure cost by 90% by making its architecture simpler, not more distributed.

O2Prime Video
Optional · Balancing act

Simplicity versus fashionable architecture

In March 2023, Amazon Prime Video’s Video Quality Analysis team described how they redesigned the system that monitors the quality of thousands of video streams.

At a glance

OrganisationAmazon Prime Video · Video Quality Analysis team
WhenPublished March 2023
ResultInfrastructure cost reduced by about 90%
Topic 03 link06 Balancing act

Before and after

BeforeAfter
DesignDistributed components orchestrated by AWS Step Functions and running on AWS LambdaAll components combined into a single process on Amazon EC2 and ECS
ProblemHit a hard scaling limit at about 5% of expected load; moving data between components was expensiveData stays in memory inside one process
CostHighAbout 90% lower

A balanced view

The post sparked wide debate. Some critics saw it as proof that microservices are overused; the author cautioned that the decision suited this workload and that architectures should be chosen case by case.

This is exactly the balancing act: cost, simplicity and scalability must be weighed for each problem rather than following fashion.
