SYNOPSIS

AI accelerates the process. Researchers lead the thinking.

The Writing and Publication Workshop GRG 2026 is a one-day intensive workshop organised by the Geotechnical Research Group (GRG), Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM). Designed specifically for postgraduate students, it empowers researchers with practical knowledge and skills to use Artificial Intelligence in scientific writing and publication.

Participants will explore AI-powered techniques supporting every stage of academic writing—from manuscript structure and literature reviews to language refinement, coherence and preparation for submission to high-quality journals.

Through practical demonstrations and hands-on exercises, participants will develop stronger manuscripts while learning to use AI ethically, verify evidence, maintain originality and preserve human scholarly judgement.

Responsible, human-led research

AI supports the writing process. Researchers remain responsible for evidence, interpretation, originality and every final publication decision.

Ethical and Responsible AI

05

Publication Readiness

Use AI-assisted workflows to evaluate manuscripts, improve research productivity and prepare confidently for submission.
