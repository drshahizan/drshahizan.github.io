SYNOPSIS

AI accelerates the process. Researchers lead the thinking.

The Writing & Publication Workshop GRG 2026 is a one-day intensive workshop organised by the Geotechnical Research Group (GRG), Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM). Designed specifically for postgraduate students, it empowers researchers with practical knowledge and skills to use Artificial Intelligence in scientific writing and publication.

Participants will explore AI-powered techniques supporting every stage of academic writing—from manuscript structure and literature reviews to language refinement, coherence and preparation for submission to high-quality journals.

Through practical demonstrations and hands-on exercises, participants will develop stronger manuscripts while learning to use AI ethically, verify evidence, maintain originality and preserve human scholarly judgement.

Responsible, human-led research

AI supports the writing process. Researchers remain responsible for evidence, interpretation, originality and every final publication decision.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Know what to expect.

Do I need a complete manuscript?

No. You may work with a research idea, article outline, selected manuscript section or full draft.

Can I bring reviewer comments?

Yes. Bring the decision letter, complete reviewer comments and the latest manuscript version after removing confidential information.

Which AI tools will be used?

The workshop introduces publication-focused generative AI, Gemini Notebook and selected research tools. The emphasis is on transferable workflows rather than one platform.

Can I upload unpublished or confidential data?

Do not upload confidential, identifiable, restricted or commercially sensitive material. Use anonymised or non-sensitive extracts only when permitted.

Will AI write the article for me?

No. AI supports analysis, drafting and review. The researcher remains responsible for evidence, interpretation, originality and the final manuscript.

Will the materials remain available?

The website provides continuing access to the publication prompt libraries, tools and curated learning resources.
