PRACTICAL CHECKLISTS

Use these checkpoints before submission

Journal Selection

Check scope, readership, article type, recent themes, indexing claims and author requirements.

Quality Review

Verify alignment, reporting completeness, claims, citations, ethics and reproducibility.

Submission Package

Prepare the manuscript, cover letter, highlights, declarations and supplementary files.

Reviewer Response

Track every comment, revision, evidence-based rationale and manuscript location.

Responsible use: Remove confidential information, check every source and follow the target journal’s AI-use and disclosure policy.
