PUBLICATION JOURNEY

Six phases that mirror real journal publishing

01 · POSITION

Plan the Article

Clarify purpose, audience, novelty, contribution and storyline.

02 · DEVELOP

Build the Manuscript

Develop every section around verified evidence and journal logic.

03 · STRENGTHEN

Review Quality

Audit alignment, reporting, integrity, claims and reproducibility.

04 · SELECT

Evaluate Journal Fit

Assess scope, readership, article type and mismatch risks.

05 · SUBMIT

Prepare the Package

Complete the cover letter, declarations, highlights and final checks.

06 · RESPOND

Revise & Resubmit

Classify comments, revise with evidence and verify every response.

Human Verification at Every Stage

Researchers remain accountable for evidence, interpretation, confidentiality, citations and every final publication decision.

HANDS-ON ACTIVITIES

What participants will do

PART 1

Position and Develop

  • Configure the AI role, rules and output
  • Define the publication angle and contribution
  • Build the article storyline and IMRaD outline
  • Use Gemini Notebook for evidence synthesis
  • Strengthen a selected manuscript section
PART 2

Review, Submit and Respond

  • Run a manuscript quality and integrity audit
  • Evaluate journal–manuscript fit
  • Prepare the submission package
  • Build a reviewer response matrix
  • Verify the revised manuscript before resubmission
