Frequently asked questions

Questions about joining the committee

Who can apply?
Academic staff holding a PhD. General Chair is open to Professors only. Publication Chair and Technical Program Chair are open to Associate Professors and Professors. Technical Program Committee Member is open to Professors, Associate Professors and Dr (PhD holders). See Positions & eligibility.
How much time does the role require?
The role is advisory and flexible. The organizing team handles submission management, peer-review coordination, registration, publication formatting and author correspondence.
Can I apply to both conferences?
Yes. Submit one application for each conference. Each email address may hold one application per conference.
Can I apply for more than one position in the same conference?
Choose the most senior position you are eligible for. If it is already filled, the organizing team may offer you another position or place you on the reserve list.
Do I need to be an IEEE member?
No. The form asks for your IEEE membership status for records only.
Why is a photo required?
Committee members are listed on the conference website. If you provide a personal or university webpage, the photo is optional. Without a webpage, please upload a photo.
What do committee members receive?
Discounted paper submission fees for you and your students, a certificate of appreciation, and a registration fee waiver for conference participation.
A position shows "Filled". Can I still apply?
Yes. Your application will be considered for the reserve list or for other open positions.
How will I know the outcome?
The organizing team reviews every application and contacts you by email.
I made a mistake in my application. What should I do?
A second application with the same email for the same conference will not be accepted. Please contact the organizing team by email with the correction.

Ready to apply?

It takes about five minutes.
