THE IDEA

AI skills become more valuable when they work together.

This workshop connects responsible AI use with practical digital production. Participants explore how to frame a task, shape a message, create visual content, learn from data and build a simple service workflow.

Each day adds a new piece to a portfolio. By the end, participants can explain the problem their work addresses and show how the pieces fit together.

THE INTEGRATING PROJECT

One Malaysian case.
Five days of progress.

Participants work through a fictional Johor homestay and local-experience business. They develop a responsible AI workflow, create promotional content, analyse 6,000 simulated visitor sessions by country and month, publish a service website and prototype a booking application.

Ground the practice in local service needs.
