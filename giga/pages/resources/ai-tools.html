OPTIONAL EXPLORATION

Where options may fit

Follow the step-by-step exercise instructions. These examples show where additional tools could be explored if time and access allow.

DAY 01Plan and prompt

Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude or another assistant to clarify a task and improve prompts.

DAY 02Make media

Google Flow or Runway for clips; Google Vids or CapCut for editing; Suno or ElevenLabs for audio.

DAY 03Design promotion

Stitch or Figma Make for screen concepts; Firefly and Canva for the homestay brochure and banner.

DAY 04Understand visitors

Sheets, Excel or Julius AI for synthetic visitor data; Apps Script for the Sheets-connected practice app.

DAY 05Build a service

Use the exercise’s Supabase workflow; coding assistants or app builders are optional ways to explore further.

Reminder: AI-generated content, data summaries and code can be wrong. Check the output, protect data and use only the tool specified by your trainer when an activity requires one.
