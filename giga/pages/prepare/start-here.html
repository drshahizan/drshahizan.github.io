BEFORE THE WORKSHOP

Prepare your workspace

01 · DEVICE

Bring a laptop

Use a laptop with a modern browser, reliable internet access, a charger and enough free space to save images, video drafts, CSV files and project folders.

02 · ACCOUNTS

Check access early

Confirm that you can access the AI, design, spreadsheet and database tools listed in the relevant exercise. Tool plans, institutional access and account requirements can differ.

Do not wait until the workshop to discover that a tool requires an account or approval.

03 · FILES

Make one project folder

Create a folder named Mersing-Homestay-Workshop. Inside it, make folders for Day 1, Day 2, Day 3, Day 4 and Day 5. Save prompts, drafts and final exports in the matching folder.

04 · SAFE PRACTICE

Use fictional details

The homestay is a fictional case. Use only the facts supplied in the exercises. Never enter real guest names, phone numbers, booking details, passwords, API keys or other sensitive information into prompts or practice apps.

05 · ACCESSIBILITY

Prepare for review

Bring headphones for audio/video activities if available. Keep captions on where possible, use readable text sizes, and save an export or screenshot when the guide asks you to check the result.

06 · WORKSHOP DETAILS

Check your invitation

Refer to the organiser’s latest invitation for the confirmed date, venue or meeting link, attendance instructions and support contact. These details are not published in this guide.

WHEN YOU GET STUCK

Quick troubleshooting

The AI output includes facts that are not in the case.

Do not use the unsupported claim. Ask the tool to list assumptions, remove unverified details, and mark missing information as a placeholder. Check the result against the case brief before publishing.

The prompt produces a different format than requested.

Repeat the required format, audience, length and headings at the end of your prompt. Ask for one small revision at a time, then copy the final text into your project notes.

The dashboard is empty or a chart looks incorrect.

Confirm the CSV was imported, check that the date and country columns were recognised, clear filters, and verify that the selected month contains records. Compare the chart total with the filtered row count.

The Google Sheets or Supabase app cannot save a test request.

Follow the relevant setup steps in the Day 4 or Day 5 guide in order. Check deployment status, table/column names and access rules. Use only fictional test entries and never paste a secret key into a public website.

A tool asks for payment, a card or access that I do not have.

Do not purchase a plan for the exercise. Tell the facilitator and use the suggested alternative or observe the demonstration. Tool availability can vary by account and institution.

My output does not look exactly like the example.

Examples show the expected type of result, not a single required design. Check that your output meets the brief, is readable, uses verified information and can be explained to another person.

PLAIN-LANGUAGE GLOSSARY

Key terms used in the workshop

PromptInstructions and context given to a generative AI tool to request an output.
Generative AIAI that creates or transforms content such as text, images, audio, video or code.
DashboardA visual display of selected measures and charts used to explore data.
Static websiteA website made from pages and assets that do not need a database to display.
Google Apps ScriptA scripting service used in the workshop to connect a web form with a Google Sheet.
DatabaseAn organised store of records that an application can read or update.
SupabaseA platform used in Day 5 to practise a database-backed application.
CRUDCreate, read, update and delete: common actions for managing records.
Row Level Security (RLS)Rules that control which database rows a user or application can access.
API keyA credential that identifies or grants access to a service. Keep secret keys private.
