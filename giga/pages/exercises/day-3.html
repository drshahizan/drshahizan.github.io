THE CAMPAIGN CASE

Mersing Coastal Homestay

A fictional family-run homestay in Mersing, Johor, offers stays and local experiences. Your group will prepare a small promotional kit for one selected audience.

Use only confirmed case facts. Do not invent prices, facilities, awards, availability, contact details or booking terms. Treat generated images as illustrative concepts, not photographs of the real property.

9:00–10:30 · CLIENT BRIEF AND MOODBOARD

Set the direction
before creating visuals.

SESSION OUTPUTOne campaign brief and a one-page mini visual guide.
3.115 MIN

Read and verify the case brief

Separate known facts from details that still need confirmation.

  1. Write down the business name and location.
  2. Record the two confirmed offer areas: stays and local experiences.
  3. Create a “do not invent” list for prices, facilities, contacts and availability.
  4. Mark unknown information as a placeholder, such as [PACKAGE DETAILS].
SaveA fact sheet with confirmed details and placeholders
Prompt to use in this stepChatGPT · organise facts
You are helping a beginner prepare a fictional homestay promotion. Organise only these confirmed facts: the case name is Mersing Coastal Homestay; it is family-run; it is in Mersing, Johor; it offers stays and local experiences. Make a two-column table called Confirmed and Needs confirmation. Put prices, facilities, exact activities, availability, contact details and booking terms under Needs confirmation. Do not make up any information.
Expected output

The table separates confirmed facts (family-run; Mersing, Johor; stays and local experiences) from details to verify (prices, facilities, specific activities, availability and booking information).

3.220 MIN

Choose one audience and goal

Make the promotion specific enough to guide image and layout choices.

  1. Choose families, couples or a group of friends.
  2. Write one sentence about what this audience may be looking for.
  3. Choose one campaign goal: introduce the stay or invite an enquiry.
  4. Choose one primary action for the reader.
SaveAudience · need · campaign goal · call to action
Prompt to use in this stepChatGPT · choose an audience
For this fictional case only: Mersing Coastal Homestay is a family-run homestay in Mersing, Johor offering stays and local experiences. Compare families, couples and groups of friends as possible audiences. For each, suggest one general travel need and one suitable promotion goal. Do not invent facilities, prices or specific activities. End with one recommended audience and a one-sentence reason.
Expected output

Example decision: Audience — families. General need — plan a trip together. Goal — introduce the stay and local experiences. Call to action — view package information [details to be confirmed].

3.320 MIN

Write the campaign message

Create one simple message that can appear consistently across the brochure and banner.

  1. Draft three short headline options.
  2. Choose one headline that fits the audience and brief.
  3. Write one supporting sentence using confirmed facts only.
  4. Choose a short call to action; leave contact or booking details as a placeholder.
Example direction“Discover Mersing at your own pace” · “Explore the stay”
Prompt to use in this stepChatGPT · write the campaign message
Write three short campaign headline options for a fictional family-run homestay in Mersing, Johor. Confirmed offer: stays and local experiences. Audience: [insert your selected audience]. Do not claim facilities, prices, awards or specific activities. For each headline, add one factual supporting sentence and one short call to action using a placeholder for enquiry details. Keep the tone warm, clear and professional.
Expected output

Example message: Headline — “Discover Mersing at your own pace”; support — “Explore a family-run stay and local experiences in Mersing, Johor”; call to action — “Make an enquiry: [contact details].”

3.430 MIN

Build the moodboard and mini guide

Agree on a repeatable visual style before generating images.

  1. Select three mood words, such as calm, welcoming and local.
  2. Choose two or three colours and one heading/body type pairing.
  3. Choose the image treatment: natural light, realistic textures and uncluttered composition.
  4. Record logo/wordmark direction and rules for spacing, contrast and image use.
SaveOne-page moodboard and mini visual guide
Prompt to use in this stepChatGPT · create the visual guide
Create a beginner-friendly one-page mini visual guide for the fictional Mersing Coastal Homestay campaign. Use these mood words: calm, welcoming, local. Suggest a palette with 3 HEX colours, one readable heading font and one body font, photo direction, spacing rule, and text contrast rule. Do not create a logo that copies a real brand. Return the guide as a compact table and make the palette suitable for both a brochure and a banner.
Expected output

Example guide: calm, welcoming and local; soft sea blue, warm sand and leaf green; bold sans-serif heading; readable sans-serif body; natural-light photography; dark text on a light background.

10:45–12:30 · PROMPTING AND IMAGE GENERATION

Generate a coherent
set of campaign images.

SESSION OUTPUTThree selected concept images and a prompt log.
3.520 MIN

Build the image prompt

Use the same brief and visual guide for every image in the set.

  1. Describe the subject and fictional Mersing setting.
  2. Specify audience, mood, composition, lighting and aspect ratio.
  3. Reserve clear space for text to be added later in a design editor.
  4. Exclude words, logos, signs, prices and claims about real facilities.
UseCopy Prompt 3.1 below, then edit the audience and scene
Prompt to use in this stepChatGPT · prepare the image prompt
Write one ready-to-use image-generation prompt for a fictional homestay campaign in Mersing, Johor. Audience: [selected audience]. Mood: [three mood words]. Visual direction: [paste palette and photo style]. Scene: a general, non-specific coastal travel moment that does not imply a particular property, facility or named activity. Specify portrait 4:5, natural light, realistic textures, one clear focal point and empty space for later text. Exclude text, logos, signs, prices, identifiable businesses and unconfirmed claims. Return only the prompt.
Expected output

The output is one complete image prompt that specifies the audience, mood, visual direction, composition and exclusions.

3.645 MIN

Generate four visual concepts

Create options for one connected campaign, not four unrelated styles.

  1. Generate one hero image for the brochure cover.
  2. Generate one supporting image for a stay or welcome scene.
  3. Generate one image representing a local experience without naming an unconfirmed activity.
  4. Keep the same palette, light and visual style across the set.
SaveAt least four draft concepts in a clearly named folder
Prompt to use in this stepImage generator · create concepts
Create one photorealistic illustrative campaign image for a fictional family-run homestay in Mersing, Johor. Audience: [selected audience]. Show a general, non-specific coastal travel moment; do not depict a recognisable real property, named attraction, or unconfirmed facility or activity. Mood: calm, welcoming, local. Use [paste selected colours/style], natural light, realistic textures, and one clear focal point. Portrait 4:5 composition with clean empty space for editable text. No words, logos, signs, prices, or identifiable businesses. This is an illustrative concept, not a photograph of the actual homestay.
Expected output

Generate four concept variations with the same prompt. Change only one element at a time, such as camera angle or subject placement. Save all four and select three for the kit.

3.720 MIN

Compare and select

Use a simple review matrix instead of choosing only the most attractive image.

  1. Score each image for brief fit, visual quality and style consistency.
  2. Check faces, hands, signs, text-like marks and unrealistic details.
  3. Reject images that imply an unconfirmed facility or actual property view.
  4. Select one hero image and two supporting images.
SaveThree selected images plus one reason for each selection
Prompt to use in this stepChatGPT · compare images
I have attached four AI-generated concept images for the same fictional Mersing homestay campaign. Compare only what is visible. Score each image from 1–5 for (a) fit with the brief, (b) visual clarity, (c) consistency with the selected calm/welcoming/local style, and (d) suitability for editable text placement. Do not infer that a facility or activity exists. Return a table, note visible image-generation problems, and recommend one hero image plus two supporting images. If you cannot inspect an attachment, say so.
Expected output

Example matrix: image A — hero because the subject is clear and there is room for a headline; images B and C — supporting images. Record one reason for each choice.

3.815 MIN

Keep a prompt and asset log

Make the visual work easy to review and reproduce.

  1. Save the prompt beside each selected image.
  2. Record which image tool and settings were used.
  3. Rename files by role: hero, supporting-01 and supporting-02.
  4. Mark each image “AI-generated concept”.
SavePrompt log and labelled image asset folder
Prompt to use in this stepChatGPT · create an asset log
Create a simple asset log template for a beginner's AI image exercise. Use columns: file name, role (hero/supporting-01/supporting-02), image prompt, tool, date, edits made, selected or rejected, and note that the image is an AI-generated concept. Return a blank table followed by one clearly labelled example row with fictional placeholder values. Do not invent real tool usage or dates.
Expected output

Example log row: hero-mersing-01.png | Hero | [saved prompt] | [tool name] | [date] | 4:5 crop | Selected | AI-generated concept image.

1:30–3:00 · IMAGE REFINEMENT

Refine the selected assets
for real layouts.

SESSION OUTPUTPolished hero/supporting images and one visual mockup.
3.925 MIN

Refine one image at a time

Make controlled changes so participants can see what each instruction does.

  1. Choose the hero image.
  2. Identify one issue: crop, lighting, clutter or space for text.
  3. Ask the image tool to change only that issue.
  4. Compare with the original and keep the version that best fits the brief.
RuleDo not change the scene into an unverified facility or make it look like a real property photo
Prompt to use in this stepImage editor or ChatGPT · refine an image
Refine the attached selected concept image for the same fictional Mersing homestay campaign. Preserve the subject, general setting, people and camera angle. Change only this one item: [choose one: soften the light / simplify the background / add clean negative space on the left]. Keep the selected campaign palette and calm, welcoming, local mood. Do not add text, logos, signs, prices, identifiable businesses, facilities or new claims. Return one revised illustrative concept image.
Expected output

Save both the original and edited versions. Example file names: hero-original.png and hero-refined.png. Requested change: add clear space on the left for the headline.

3.1025 MIN

Prepare crops and background edits

Make the assets usable in both brochure and banner formats.

  1. Crop one version for the A4 brochure and one for a wide banner.
  2. Keep the main subject visible and leave space for editable text.
  3. Remove or simplify a background only if it improves readability.
  4. Check that the edit does not create an inaccurate visual claim.
SaveOne portrait/print crop and one landscape crop
Prompt to use in this stepImage editor · prepare crops
Prepare two crops from the attached illustrative concept image for the same campaign: (1) portrait 4:5 for an A4 brochure and (2) wide landscape 16:9 for a digital banner. Keep the main subject visible, preserve natural proportions, and leave clear space for editable text. Do not add or alter factual details. If direct cropping is unavailable, give me simple crop instructions instead.
Expected output

Two assets: a 4:5 portrait crop for the brochure and a 16:9 landscape crop for the banner. Check that the subject is not cut off and the text area remains clear.

3.1125 MIN

Test the visual system in a mockup

Preview the same image set in the intended promotion layouts.

  1. Place the hero image in an A4 brochure template.
  2. Place a supporting image in a landscape banner template.
  3. Check colour, image treatment and headline placement across both.
  4. Adjust the visual guide if the pieces do not feel like one campaign.
SaveOne brochure mockup and one banner mockup
Prompt to use in this stepChatGPT · plan the mockup
Give me a simple Canva layout plan for previewing one fictional Mersing homestay campaign as both an A4 portrait brochure and a landscape banner. Use the same palette and visual style in both. State the order of elements, approximate placement, and which of the three selected concept images to use. Use placeholders for package details and contact information. Do not invent facts. Keep instructions suitable for a first-time Canva user.
Expected output

Example mockup: A4 brochure with a hero image at the top, headline and supporting sentence, two information sections, package details and a call to action. Banner uses a wide image, one headline and one action.

3:15–4:30 · PROMOTIONAL GRAPHIC AND CHANNEL ADAPTATION

Assemble the promotion kit
for print and digital use.

SESSION OUTPUTOne-page A4 brochure and one landscape banner variation.
3.1235 MIN

Design a one-page A4 brochure

Use a prepared layout template so the time is spent on message, imagery and readability.

  1. Add the approved headline and supporting sentence.
  2. Place the hero image and two supporting images.
  3. Add sections for stay, local experiences and enquiry details.
  4. Use placeholders for unconfirmed package, price, contact and booking information.
  5. Keep final text editable in the design tool; do not bake text into generated images.
DeliverDigital A4 brochure, PDF export and editable source
Prompt to use in this stepChatGPT · draft brochure copy
Draft concise text for a one-page A4 brochure for the fictional Mersing Coastal Homestay. Audience: [selected audience]. Campaign headline: [approved headline]. Confirmed facts only: family-run homestay; located in Mersing, Johor; offers stays and local experiences. Provide one headline (8 words max), one supporting sentence, three section headings, one short paragraph per section, and one call to action. Put [PACKAGE DETAILS], [PRICE], [CONTACT] and [BOOKING LINK] wherever information is missing. Do not invent amenities, awards, availability, travel times, reviews or specific activities.
Expected output

The brochure copy includes a headline, supporting sentence, “The Stay,” “Local Experiences,” and “Plan Your Visit” sections, plus “Make an enquiry: [CONTACT].” Unconfirmed details remain placeholders.

3.1325 MIN

Adapt the brochure into a banner

Create a wide digital version for a website header or social media cover.

  1. Duplicate the brochure page into a wide-format canvas.
  2. Keep only the headline, one supporting line and the main call to action.
  3. Choose the crop that preserves the subject and leaves room for text.
  4. Do not simply shrink the brochure onto the banner.
DeliverOne landscape banner using the same campaign identity
Prompt to use in this stepChatGPT · adapt the banner
Adapt this approved brochure content into a landscape digital banner for the same fictional Mersing homestay campaign. Keep the same visual identity and one headline. Include only one short supporting line and one call to action. Recommend a simple left-to-right layout, a safe text area and the best wide crop from the selected images. Do not add any facts. Keep all copy editable in Canva.
Expected output

The banner has one landscape image, one readable headline, one short supporting line and one call to action. Small brochure details are removed.

3.1415 MIN

Run the five-second test

Ask a partner to review the two outputs without explanation.

  1. Ask: What is being promoted?
  2. Ask: Who is it for?
  3. Ask: What should the viewer do next?
  4. Fix the most confusing element and save the revised version.
RecordOne peer comment and one revision made
Prompt to use in this stepPeer review · five-second test
Create a five-second peer-review checklist for a beginner's homestay brochure and banner. Ask the reviewer to look at each design for five seconds, then answer: What is being promoted? Who is it for? What should the viewer do next? Add one question about readability and one space for a specific improvement. Do not judge factual details that are not shown.
Expected output

Example review form: What is being promoted? Who is it for? What should the viewer do next? What is hardest to read? What is one suggested improvement?

Optional extension: adapt the banner into a 1080 × 1920 social story or WhatsApp status. If more time is available, turn the one-page brochure into a tri-fold pamphlet.

4:30–5:00 · QUALITY CHECK AND EXPORT

Hand over a coherent
promotion kit.

Before exporting, confirm the facts, visual quality and editable files. Use a consistent naming structure so the group can reuse the assets.

Final checklist

  1. Check spelling, readability, contrast and text hierarchy.
  2. Confirm no invented prices, facilities, contact details or claims appear.
  3. Label AI-generated images as illustrative concepts.
  4. Export the brochure as PDF and the banner as PNG or JPG.
  5. Save the editable design, prompt log and source image files.
