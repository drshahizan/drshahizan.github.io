SHARED CASE STUDY

Mersing Coastal Homestay

This fictional family-run homestay in Mersing, Johor offers stays and local experiences. The dashboard uses simulated website visit sessions to explore monthly patterns and visitor countries.

Training data only: all 6,000 sessions and values are synthetic. One row represents one website visit session, not a unique person. Do not enter real guest names, phone numbers, identity numbers, addresses or payment details into AI tools, forms or practice applications.

↓Download the 2025 visitor-session datasetCSV · 6,000 simulated sessions · 12 fields · Jan–Dec 2025 · no personal data

The file covers January–December 2025 and includes 13 countries, five channels, device types, packages and simulated enquiry outcomes. Country values are synthetic visitor-origin labels, not geolocation or verified nationality. Sessions count visits, not unique people.

View the 12 dataset fields and what they mean
FieldMeaning
session_idUnique synthetic ID for one website visit session
visit_dateSession date in YYYY-MM-DD format
monthMonth key from 2025-01 to 2025-12
visitor_countrySynthetic visitor-origin country label
country_codeTwo-letter ISO country code
channelSimulated source channel for the visit
device_typeSimulated device category
package_viewedPromotional page or package viewed
session_duration_secondsSimulated time spent in the session
enquiry_submittedWhether the session submitted an enquiry
booking_statusBlank when no enquiry; otherwise Confirmed, Pending or Cancelled
booking_value_rmFictional value for confirmed training records only

Country labels are synthetic; they do not come from IP addresses or verified visitor identities.
