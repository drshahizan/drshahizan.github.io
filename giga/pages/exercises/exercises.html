SHARED CASE STUDY

Mersing Coastal Homestay

A fictional family-run homestay in Mersing, Johor, wants to promote local stays and experiences, understand visitor patterns, and organise enquiries. Activities build from visual materials and visitor analytics into a homestay enquiry app with a Supabase booking manager.

Use simulated information only. Do not enter real guest details into AI tools, forms or practice applications.

DAY 01

Think with AI

Practise responsible AI, clear prompts, verification and an agentic service-workflow case study.

Prompt workflow + case map → DAY 02

Tell the story

Turn the Mersing case into a short video with a brief, script, storyboard, captions and showcase.

Short edited video → DAY 03

Design with AI

Create a mini visual guide, a set of concept images, an A4 brochure and a matching landscape banner.

Brochure + banner kit → DAY 04

Measure and launch

Explore 6,000 simulated visitor sessions, build a country-map dashboard with a month filter, create a static page and test a Sheets-backed enquiry app.

Dashboard + site + Sheets app → DAY 05

Build the homestay booking app

Reuse Day 3 visuals and connect the visitor enquiry form and manager actions to Supabase.
