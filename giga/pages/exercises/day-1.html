Case facts: Mersing Coastal Homestay is a fictional family-run homestay in Mersing, Johor. It offers stays and local experiences. Prices, facilities, exact activities, availability and contact details are not specified. Keep them as placeholders.
ACTIVITY 1 · RESPONSIBLE USE

Separate known facts from assumptions

Use this short exercise before asking AI to create content. It helps prevent made-up details from becoming part of the promotion.

  1. Read the case facts above.
  2. List information you can use and details that still need confirmation.
  3. Mark any personal or confidential information that must not be entered into an AI tool.
Prompt to copy · Organise the case facts
You are helping a beginner prepare a fictional homestay service. Organise only these confirmed facts: Mersing Coastal Homestay is a fictional family-run homestay in Mersing, Johor; it offers stays and local experiences. Create two lists: Confirmed facts and Needs confirmation. Put prices, facilities, exact activities, availability, booking terms and contact details under Needs confirmation. Do not invent information. Add a short reminder about not entering real guest or personal data into AI tools.
Expected outputA two-part fact sheet. Confirmed facts are limited to the case details above; all other business information remains a placeholder.
ACTIVITY 2 · PROMPT DESIGN

Turn a vague request into a useful prompt

Build a prompt using a clear task, context, audience, constraints and output format. Replace the bracketed choices before using it.

  1. Choose an audience: families, couples or groups of friends.
  2. Choose a task: draft a short introduction to the homestay case.
  3. Include the confirmed facts and clear rules against making up details.
  4. Ask for a specific output format that you can review.
Prompt to copy · Draft a short introduction
Task: Write three short introductory descriptions for a fictional homestay. Context: Mersing Coastal Homestay is family-run, located in Mersing, Johor, and offers stays and local experiences. Audience: [families / couples / groups of friends]. Constraints: Use only the confirmed facts. Do not invent facilities, prices, awards, specific activities, availability or contact details. Use placeholders for missing information. Output: a table with columns Version, Description (maximum 45 words), and Claims to verify. Use a warm, clear and professional tone.
Expected outputThree short options with a verification column. Select one, edit it, and keep a note of any claims that need checking.
ACTIVITY 3 · ITERATE AND VERIFY

Improve one response without losing accuracy

Select the best draft from Activity 2. Ask for a focused revision, then check every factual statement against the case brief.

  1. Underline factual claims and highlight adjectives that may overpromise.
  2. Ask for one change at a time: shorter, clearer, more welcoming or easier to scan.
  3. Reject details that are not supported by the fictional case facts.
  4. Save the final version and a short note describing your edits.
Prompt to copy · Refine and check
Revise the description below for clarity and a welcoming tone. Keep it under 35 words. Use only these confirmed facts: fictional family-run homestay; located in Mersing, Johor; offers stays and local experiences. Do not add amenities, prices, guarantees or specific activities. After the revised description, list each factual claim and label it Supported by the brief or Needs confirmation. Draft: [paste your selected draft]
Expected outputA shorter description plus a claim check. Your final text should contain only supported information or clearly marked placeholders.
ACTIVITY 4 · AGENTIC AI CASE STUDY

Map a service request workflow with human approval

Analyse a demonstration scenario. The purpose is to understand the workflow and safeguards, not to build or deploy a production AI agent.

  1. Start with a fictional guest enquiry asking about a stay and local experiences.
  2. Identify which approved information an assistant may consult.
  3. Mark actions an AI could draft and actions that require staff approval.
  4. Write one failure case and the safe next step.
Prompt to copy · Draft the workflow map
Create a simple workflow map in numbered steps for a fictional homestay enquiry assistant. The assistant may read an approved FAQ containing confirmed services and may draft a reply. A staff member must approve any reply before it is sent and must handle pricing, availability, booking confirmation, complaints and requests involving personal data. Include: visitor action, AI action, information source, human approval point and what to do if the answer is unknown. Do not invent homestay policies.
Expected outputA workflow showing the enquiry, approved information lookup, draft response, human review and safe hand-off when information is missing or sensitive.
