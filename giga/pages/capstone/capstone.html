THE CASE

Mersing Coastal Homestay

A fictional family-run homestay in Mersing, Johor, offers overnight stays and local experiences. The owners receive enquiries through different channels and want a clearer way to answer common questions, promote their services, understand visitor patterns and organise enquiries.

⌂
THE SERVICE CHALLENGEHow might a small Malaysian tourism business make its digital service clearer, more useful and easier to manage?

Participants work as a small digital service team. They identify the user need, choose appropriate uses for AI and create a series of prototypes. The aim is to demonstrate a connected service concept—not to claim a production-ready commercial system.

THE PROJECT, DAY BY DAY

Each topic adds a new capability.

See the full timetable →
01THINK WITH AI

Design a responsible response workflow.

Draft and refine a prompt for answering common guest enquiries. Map the information sources an agent may consult, the actions it may take and the points that require human approval.

OUTPUTReusable prompt workflow + case-study mapGuided analysis and demonstration; no production agent build.
02TELL THE STORY

Introduce the experience.

Choose a stay or local-experience offer. Define the audience, write a short script, plan scenes, prepare audio and edit a concise promotional video.

OUTPUTShort edited and exported videoShowcase the visitor need and service offer.
03DESIGN WITH AI

Create a consistent visual direction.

Build a moodboard and visual guide, generate suitable image assets, refine composition and prepare a main promotional graphic with a second-channel variation.

OUTPUTMini visual guide + images + brochure + bannerLabel concept images; verify facts and add final text in the design editor.
04MEASURE & LAUNCH

Make information useful to the owner and guest.

Analyse 6,000 synthetic visitor sessions by country and month, build a dashboard and static website, then create an Apps Script web app that stores enquiries in Google Sheets.

OUTPUTCountry map + monthly dashboard + static website + Google Sheets web appTest the visitor journey and confirm records are saved in the training Sheet.
05BUILD

Connect the homestay booking journey.

Reuse the Day 3 promotional assets and Day 4 page structure. Build a package page and enquiry form, then connect it to a Supabase manager view for simulated requests.

OUTPUTHomestay enquiry and booking appDemonstrate create, read, update and delete; validate inputs and check the approved Row Level Security policy.

TRAINING DATA

Use realistic structures.
Keep the records fictional.

Use the 6,000 visit sessions for the Day 4 dashboard. For the Day 5 Supabase booking app, use a separate small set of fictional booking requests. A visit session is not a booking.
