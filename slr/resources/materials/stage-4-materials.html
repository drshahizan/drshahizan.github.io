Manuscript Refinement and Publication Readiness

Course materials are hosted directly in this website and organised around the 2026 SLR learning journey.

1. Manuscript Refinement

Review the manuscript at three levels: methodological completeness, evidence-to-claim alignment and scholarly presentation. Check that each conclusion can be traced to the included evidence and that the Method is reproducible.

2. Reporting and Transparency

Use the relevant reporting guideline, including PRISMA where applicable. Ensure search strategies, selection decisions, study characteristics, quality assessment, synthesis method and limitations are reported clearly.

3. Journal Matching

Journal selection should consider scope, audience, article type, indexing, recent related publications, length and formatting requirements, publication model and ethical/research-integrity requirements.

4. Publication Readiness Checklist

  1. Confirm manuscript–journal scope alignment.
  2. Check author guidelines and required article structure.
  3. Verify references, DOI details, tables, figures and supplementary files.
  4. Check consistency between RQs, Method, Results and Conclusions.
  5. Complete reporting checklist and declarations.
  6. Prepare title, abstract, keywords and cover letter.
  7. Conduct a final language and formatting review.

5. Responsible Use of AI and Digital Tools

Digital tools may assist discovery, organisation, drafting and language refinement, but authors remain responsible for verification, citation accuracy, originality, confidentiality and compliance with the target journal's AI policy.
