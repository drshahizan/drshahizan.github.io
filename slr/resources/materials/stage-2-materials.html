Stage 2 · REVIEW · Integrated learning materials

Screening, Quality Assessment and Data Extraction

Course materials are hosted directly in this website and organised around the 2026 SLR learning journey.

1. Preparing the Review

Before screening, define the review scope, eligibility criteria and decision rules. The protocol should state the research questions, databases, search strategy, inclusion and exclusion criteria, screening process, quality assessment approach and extraction fields.

2. Deduplication and Screening

  1. Combine records from all databases.
  2. Remove exact and near duplicates while retaining an audit trail.
  3. Screen titles and abstracts against the eligibility criteria.
  4. Retrieve full texts for potentially eligible studies.
  5. Apply the same criteria during full-text screening and record exclusion reasons.
  6. Maintain counts required for the PRISMA flow diagram.

4. Quality Assessment

Quality appraisal should use criteria suitable for the included study designs. Define the checklist and scoring/decision rule before assessment. Keep the appraisal separate from personal preference and document all judgements.

5. Data Extraction

Create a standard extraction matrix. Common fields include citation, year, research objective, context, sample/data, method, variables or constructs, technology/algorithm, evaluation measures, principal findings, limitations and evidence relevant to each research question.

6. Evidence Synthesis Preparation

Group extracted evidence by research question, concept, method, outcome or theme. Do not begin narrative conclusions until the extraction matrix is sufficiently complete.
