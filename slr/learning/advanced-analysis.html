Phase 3

Advanced Analysis

Transform extracted evidence into themes, visual insight, collaborative decisions, and report-ready outputs.

Synthesise · Visualise · Collaborate · Report
01

Thematic Analysis

Develop codes, categories, themes, and subthemes while retaining links to supporting studies.

Core activities

  • Create and refine an auditable codebook
  • Map themes to research questions and studies
  • Record evidence strength and analytic notes

Output: Theme matrix, narrative synthesis, and research gap map.

02

Interactive Visualisation

Explore patterns in publication years, countries, methods, theories, datasets, themes, and quality scores.

Interactions

  • Filter by year, database, quartile, or theme
  • Select a chart element to inspect supporting studies
  • Export publication-ready PNG or SVG figures

Output: Evidence maps and manuscript-ready visualisations.

03

Team Collaboration

Coordinate screening, appraisal, extraction, and synthesis across a research team.

Team controls

  • Assign articles and review tasks
  • Track progress and reviewer disagreements
  • Define owner, supervisor, reviewer, extractor, and viewer roles

Output: Transparent workload, contribution, and progress records.

04

Change Log

Maintain an audit trail for metadata, decisions, scores, extraction data, and duplicate resolution.

Recorded details

  • User, date, action, and article ID
  • Previous value, new value, and reason
  • History filtering and restoration support

Output: Reproducible record of all material changes.

05

Report Generation

Assemble verified project data into structured protocol, methods, results, tables, and appendices.

Generated sections

  • Search, selection, quality, and extraction methods
  • PRISMA summary and study characteristics
  • Theme summaries, evidence tables, and research gaps

Output: Reviewable DOCX, PDF, Markdown, or HTML drafts.

✓
Researcher judgement remains essential.

AI may assist with coding and summarisation, but every theme, interpretation, statistic, and generated paragraph must be verified against the underlying studies.
