Step 08 of 10

Assess Study Quality

Judge how much confidence each included study can contribute to the review findings.

Create a fit-for-purpose checklist

  1. Select an established appraisal tool or justify adapted criteria.
  2. Cover study aims, design, data, analysis, validity, and reporting.
  3. Define Yes, Partial, No, and Not Applicable scoring rules.
  4. Pilot the checklist and calibrate the reviewers.
  5. Assess studies independently where possible.
  6. Resolve disagreements and calculate the final score.
  7. Apply the predefined inclusion or weighting rule.
Important distinction

Reporting quality and methodological quality are related but not identical. Do not reward a well-written paper for an unsuitable design.

Quality check

  • Questions match the included research designs.
  • Scoring rules are explicit and reproducible.
  • The threshold was set before final scoring.
  • Sensitivity to lower-quality evidence is discussed.

Required output

Quality Assessment Table

  • Assessment questions
  • Scoring guidance
  • Scores by study
  • Reviewer agreement
  • Final decision
