Design the Search Strategy

Translate your research questions into sensitive, precise, and reproducible database queries.

Build the query systematically

  1. Extract two to four core concepts from the RQs.
  2. List synonyms, abbreviations, spelling variants, and related terms.
  3. Combine alternatives with OR and concepts with AND.
  4. Use quotation marks, wildcards, proximity operators, and field codes appropriately.
  5. Translate the master query into Scopus, WoS, and other selected syntax.
  6. Pilot the query against known relevant articles and refine it.
Boolean structure

(“systematic review” OR “evidence synthesis”) AND (“artificial intelligence” OR “machine learning”) AND (education OR learning)

Quality check

  • All key concepts have documented synonyms.
  • The query retrieves known relevant studies.
  • Database-specific syntax is correct.
  • Every final query is saved exactly as run.
