Quick distinction

Database, publisher, or index?

Bibliographic index

Web of Science and Scopus index publication records and citations.

Publisher platform

ScienceDirect and SpringerLink provide access to published content.

Journal metric source

JCR and SJR provide metrics reported with a year and subject category.

Protocol essentials

Plan before searching

Question frameworks

Use PICO, PICOC, SPIDER, CIMO, or Population–Concept–Context to structure the review question.

Eligibility and quality

Define databases, years, document types, inclusion criteria, exclusion criteria, and appraisal rules in advance.

Responsible reporting

Follow PRISMA 2020, preserve an audit trail, report limitations, and verify every AI-assisted output.
