Step 10 of 10

Write and Submit the SLR Article

Assemble the review into a coherent manuscript, verify its reporting, and prepare a complete submission package.

Recommended article structure

  1. Title and abstract: identify the topic, review type, method, main findings, and contribution.
  2. Introduction: establish the problem, gap, motivation, objectives, and RQs.
  3. Background: define essential concepts without duplicating the results.
  4. Methodology: report the protocol, search, screening, quality assessment, extraction, and synthesis.
  5. Results: present PRISMA counts, study characteristics, and findings by RQ.
  6. Discussion: interpret patterns, implications, gaps, and differences from previous reviews.
  7. Limitations: discuss search, selection, data, and review-process constraints.
  8. Conclusion: answer the purpose and state the contribution and future direction.

Before submission

  • Use the PRISMA 2020 checklist and flow diagram.
  • Match the journal's aims, scope, format, and word limit.
  • Verify every citation, table, figure, count, and supplementary file.
  • Confirm authorship, declarations, acknowledgements, and data availability.
  • Prepare a concise cover letter explaining fit and contribution.

Final output

Submission-ready SLR Article

  • Complete manuscript
  • PRISMA checklist
  • PRISMA flow diagram
  • Supplementary files
  • Cover letter
  • Submission checklist
