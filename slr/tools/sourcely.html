DISCOVERY & DATABASES · STAGE 1 · DISCOVER

Sourcely

Finds, filters and summarises relevant scholarly sources from keywords or existing text.

Purpose: Source DiscoveryStage fit: Stage 1Relevance: Context-dependent

How it can support an SLR

Use this tool to locate candidate sources, explore terminology or trace a verified seed paper. Distinguish an indexing database from a publisher platform or discovery assistant. Record the search source and verify each candidate in its original database record.

Access and features

Open the provider’s official website to check current access, sign-in requirements, institutional availability, and product features. The tool is introduced here through its SLR-relevant purpose; availability and interfaces may change.

Open official tool website ↗

Hands-on practice

  1. Choose one task from your current SLR Stage and open the tool’s official site.
  2. Use a narrow, non-sensitive example: a research question, one verified seed article, or an approved sample record.
  3. Try the tool’s relevant search, organisation, analysis, writing or visualisation feature.
  4. Compare the result with the original article, database record, review protocol or source data.
  5. Decide what to keep, revise or reject, then record the decision in the relevant Stage worksheet.

Verification: Check the source coverage, export settings, licence and current platform guidance before relying on outputs.

What to record in your SLR

  • Tool name and task performed
  • Search query, prompt, seed paper or input dataset, where relevant
  • Date used and key result or output
  • How the result was verified and what you accepted, changed or rejected

Do not report a database search or a screening decision unless you actually performed and documented it.
