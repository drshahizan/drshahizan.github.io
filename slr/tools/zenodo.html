RESEARCH DATA · STAGE 4 · SHARE SLR DATA WITH A DOI

Zenodo

A free, general-purpose research repository run by CERN with OpenAIRE. Every published record receives a DOI, and new versions are linked under one concept DOI.

Purpose: Data Repository with DOIStage fit: Stage 4Relevance: Core / useful

How it can support an SLR

Journals increasingly ask for a data availability statement. For an SLR, the shareable data are your full search strings, screening log, extraction sheet and quality assessment file. Depositing them on Zenodo gives one citable DOI for the supplementary material.

Key facts (checked 18 November 2026)

  • Up to 50 GB and 100 files per record by default; larger quotas can be requested.
  • Published records cannot simply be deleted; to change files, create a new version.
  • You can reserve a DOI before publishing, so it can be written into the manuscript first.
  • Sign-in with ORCID is supported.

Step-by-step

  1. Prepare files: search-strategies.pdf, screening-log.xlsx, data-extraction.xlsx, quality-assessment.xlsx, a PRISMA checklist, and a short README.txt explaining each file.
  2. Remove anything you may not share: full texts of copyrighted papers, personal data, or reviewer comments.
  3. Create a new upload, choose resource type Dataset, add title, authors (with ORCID), description and licence (CC BY 4.0 is common).
  4. Reserve the DOI, add it to your data availability statement, and publish when the journal or co-authors agree (you can also restrict access until then).
  5. After acceptance, link the record to the article DOI under Related works.
