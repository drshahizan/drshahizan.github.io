OSF (Open Science Framework)

A free platform from the Center for Open Science for organising a research project, registering protocols and sharing materials. Public projects and registrations can receive a DOI.

Purpose: Project Space and RegistrationStage fit: Stage 1, Stage 4Relevance: Core / useful

How it can support an SLR

Use OSF in two ways: register your SLR protocol (Stage 1) when PROSPERO does not cover your topic, which is common in computing and education; and share review materials (Stage 4) such as search strings and extraction forms.

Key facts (checked 18 November 2026)

  • OSF Storage per project or component: 5 GB if private, 50 GB if public; individual files up to 5 GB.
  • Projects are private by default; you choose what to make public.
  • Registrations are frozen, time-stamped copies, which is what reviewers look for in a protocol registration.

Step-by-step

  1. Create a project for the review and add co-authors as contributors.
  2. Upload the protocol (RQs, eligibility criteria, sources, search strategy, screening, appraisal and synthesis plans).
  3. Choose Registrations → New registration and an appropriate template for a systematic review protocol; submit to create a time-stamped registration.
  4. Cite the registration (DOI or URL) in the Methods and the Abstract (PRISMA 2020 item 24).
  5. At the end, upload the final data files, make the relevant components public, and create a DOI for the data availability statement.
