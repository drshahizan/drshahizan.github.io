WRITING, CITATION & PUBLISHING · STAGE 4 · ACADEMIC LANGUAGE

Writefull

Academic language feedback inside Microsoft Word and Overleaf, trained on published research articles. The free plan has a daily quota; Premium removes it.

Purpose: Academic Language FeedbackStage fit: Stage 4Relevance: Core / useful

How it can support an SLR

Use Writefull in Stage 4 to polish grammar, word choice and academic phrasing after the content is final. Because it works inside Word and Overleaf, you do not have to copy the manuscript into another site.

Access (checked 18 November 2026)

  • Available as a Word add-in (Word 2016 or later, desktop and web) and inside Overleaf.
  • The free plan gives limited daily language suggestions and AI writing features; Premium gives unlimited use.
  • Some features (for example TeXGPT for LaTeX code) are Overleaf-only.

Step-by-step

  1. Word: Insert → Get Add-ins, search “Writefull”, add it, then sign in. Overleaf: open the project and enable Writefull from the editor toolbar.
  2. Run language feedback on one section at a time, starting with the Abstract and Methods.
  3. Accept only edits that keep the meaning. Reject changes to technical terms, construct names and quoted search strings.
  4. Do not use paraphrasing or abstract-generation features to create content; use them, if at all, only on text you wrote, and disclose AI use as your target journal requires (AI Disclosure Generator).

Cautions

  • Language tools can change hedging (“suggests” → “shows”). Re-check claims against your synthesis.
  • Check your institution’s and the journal’s policy before uploading unpublished manuscripts to any cloud service.
