WRITING, CITATION & PUBLISHING · STAGE 4 · CHECK JOURNAL INDEXING

Scopus Source List and Discontinued Titles

Elsevier’s downloadable Scopus source title list (Excel), updated monthly, including the list of sources whose Scopus coverage has been discontinued.

Purpose: Journal Indexing CheckStage fit: Stage 4Relevance: Core / useful

How it can support an SLR

Before submitting, confirm that your target journal is currently indexed. A journal that advertises Scopus indexing may have been discontinued. Content already indexed stays in Scopus, but new articles are no longer added, which matters for promotion and graduation requirements that count Scopus-indexed publications.

Step-by-step

  1. Open the Scopus content page and download the Source title list (Excel). It includes the discontinued sources.
  2. Search the main list for the journal’s ISSN (more reliable than the title). Check “Active or Inactive” and coverage years.
  3. Search the discontinued sheet for the same ISSN. If it appears, note the date and reason.
  4. In Scopus (if your library subscribes), open Sources, search the title, and check the latest CiteScore and quartile for your subject area.
  5. Record the check with the date in your journal shortlist (Stage 4 Step 5).

Cautions

  • A journal can be listed as discontinued on Scopus.com before it appears in the downloadable list; check both if in doubt.
  • Indexing is not quality on its own. Also use Think. Check. Submit. ↗ and the Web of Science Master Journal List.
  • Never rely on indexing claims on the journal’s own website or in an invitation email.
