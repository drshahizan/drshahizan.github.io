PAPER VISUALISATION · STAGE 3 · INTERACTIVE CHARTS

Flourish

A browser-based tool for interactive and animated charts, maps and network graphs. Projects stay private until published; published visualisations are public on the free plan.

Purpose: Interactive ChartsStage fit: Stage 3Relevance: Core / useful

How it can support an SLR

Flourish suits conference slides, posters and online supplementary material: for example a clickable map of study locations, a Sankey diagram from methods to outcomes, or a network of themes. For the article itself, a static figure is usually required.

Privacy on the free plan (checked 18 November 2026)

  • Your data and projects stay private until you publish.
  • Once published, a free visualisation is publicly visible on a Flourish page that search engines can index.
  • Password protection, removal of the Flourish attribution and team features are on paid plans.

Step-by-step

  1. Pick a template (bar chart race is rarely suitable for an SLR; prefer bar, map, Sankey or network).
  2. Upload or paste your summary data and map the columns.
  3. Simplify: one message per chart, readable labels, colour-blind-safe colours.
  4. For the article, download a static image. Publish only when you are happy for the data to be public.
