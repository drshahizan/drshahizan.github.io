PAPER VISUALISATION · STAGE 3 · CHARTS FOR RESULTS

Datawrapper

A browser-based tool for clean charts, maps and tables without coding. The free plan allows unlimited charts and PNG export.

Purpose: Charts Without CodeStage fit: Stage 3Relevance: Core / useful

How it can support an SLR

Use Datawrapper for Results figures such as studies per year, studies by country (a world map), or methods by domain. It produces consistent, readable charts quickly from a pasted table.

Access (checked 18 November 2026)

  • Free plan: unlimited charts, maps and tables; export as PNG; a “Created with Datawrapper” line is added.
  • PDF and SVG export are paid features, but Datawrapper offers free PDF/SVG export to students, teachers and academic researchers on request to its support team.
  • Charts stay private until you publish them.

Step-by-step

  1. Prepare a small summary table (e.g. Year | Studies). The Study Characteristics Table Builder can give you the counts.
  2. Create a new chart, paste the table, and choose a simple chart type: column for years, bar for categories, choropleth map for countries.
  3. Remove decoration: one colour, no 3D, direct value labels where helpful, axis starting at zero.
  4. Write the denominator in the title or note, e.g. “Included studies by year (n = 26)”.
  5. Export PNG at high resolution (or PDF/SVG if granted) and check the journal’s figure requirements.

Cautions

  • Do not publish charts made from unpublished data before your article is accepted; export without publishing.
  • Keep the attribution line on the free plan unless you have a plan that removes it.
