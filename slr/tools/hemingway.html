Hemingway Editor

A free web-based readability checker. It highlights hard-to-read and very hard-to-read sentences, passive voice and adverbs, and reports a readability grade.

Purpose: Readability CheckStage fit: Stage 4Relevance: Core / useful

How it can support an SLR

SLR sections often accumulate very long sentences, especially the Discussion. Paste one paragraph at a time to find sentences that need splitting. It does not check grammar or academic conventions.

Step-by-step

  1. Open the web editor and paste one paragraph (not the whole manuscript).
  2. Look first at sentences highlighted as very hard to read. Split them into two sentences, each with one idea.
  3. Treat passive voice flags with judgement: Methods sections legitimately use passive voice (“Records were screened…”).
  4. Ignore the grade target for technical terms you must keep. Academic prose usually scores higher than general web text; aim for clarity, not a number.

Cautions

  • The free web version does not save your text; keep the manuscript in Word or Overleaf.
  • Paid “Plus” features include AI rewriting. If you use them, check meaning and disclose as required.
  • Use SLR Language Checker for SLR-specific problems (overclaiming, missing denominators, vague methods).
