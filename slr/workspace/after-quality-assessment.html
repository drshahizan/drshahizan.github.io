SLR Workshop 2026 · Stage 3 & 4 selection

After Quality Assessment: Prepare Your Submission

Finished your quality assessment? Follow these nine steps to finalise your SLR draft, quality assessment Excel file and PRISMA 2020 diagram, then submit them for Stage 3 & 4 selection.

Deadline: Monday, 30 November 2026, 5:00 PM (Malaysia time)

Late submissions will not be accepted. Steps 6 and 8 are part of the completeness check: a submission that fails them is not reviewed.

0 / 9steps completed · saved in this browser
Step 1

Finalise your included studies

Apply your quality assessment decisions to confirm which studies stay in the review.

  • Apply the criteria and the score threshold you defined in your protocol.
  • Record a reason for every study excluded at this stage (for example “Low quality: score below threshold”).
  • Keep the excluded studies in your file; do not delete them.
Output: Final list of included studiesStage 2 · Quality appraisal →
Step 2

Update your PRISMA 2020 diagram

Make the flow diagram reflect the final decisions.

  • Add the number of studies excluded after quality assessment, with reasons.
  • Update the final number of included studies.
  • Export the diagram as PDF or PNG.
Output: Final PRISMA 2020 flow diagramPRISMA Flow Builder →Editable PRISMA diagram (DOCX) ↓
Step 3

Tidy your quality assessment Excel file

Make the file easy for a reviewer to understand without you explaining it.

  • Use one row per article, with an ID, citation and year.
  • Add one column per quality criterion, then the total score, the decision and the reason.
  • Add a “Read me” sheet naming the appraisal tool, the scoring scale and the threshold you used.
Output: A clear, self-explanatory Excel fileSLR Extraction Matrix (XLSX) ↓
Step 4Recommended

Extract data from the included studies

Collect the information that answers your research questions. This gives you material for preliminary results.

  • Extract year, country, setting, method, sample and main findings for each included study.
  • Add the fields your research questions need (for example techniques, datasets or outcomes).
  • Mark missing information as “not reported” rather than leaving cells empty.
Output: Extraction matrix for your included studiesStage 2 · Data extraction →SLR Extraction Matrix (XLSX) ↓
Step 5

Update your SLR article draft

Bring the draft up to date with what you have done so far.

  • Introduction: background, problem, existing reviews, research gap and research questions.
  • Method: databases, search strings, eligibility criteria, selection process and quality assessment (tool, criteria, who assessed, how disagreements were resolved, how scores were used).
  • Preliminary results: overview of included studies and a summary table of quality assessment results.
  • Core figures and tables: PRISMA diagram, publications per year, characteristics of included studies and quality assessment results (see the gallery for examples).
  • A short draft abstract.
Output: Updated SLR article draftSLR Manuscript Template (DOCX) ↓Writing guide →Figure & table gallery →Excel figure templates ↓
Step 6Completeness check

Check that the three documents agree

This is part of the completeness check. Inconsistent counts stop a submission from being reviewed.

  • The number of included studies is the same in the PRISMA diagram, the Excel file and the draft.
  • The databases and search dates in the draft match your search log.
  • Study names and years are written the same way in all three documents.
Output: Three consistent documents
Step 7

Self-assess against the rubric

Read your draft as a reviewer would.

  • Score yourself from 1 to 5 on each of the five criteria.
  • Improve the weakest criterion first, especially Search & PRISMA and Quality assessment (25% each).
Output: A stronger draftAssessment rubric →
Step 8Completeness check

Prepare and share your files

Make sure the reviewer can open every file.

  • Name files clearly, for example MatricNo_Name_SLR_Draft.docx, MatricNo_QA.xlsx, MatricNo_PRISMA.pdf.
  • Upload each file to Google Drive or OneDrive.
  • Set access to Anyone with the link · Commenter and copy each link.
  • Test each link in a private (Incognito) browser window.
Output: Three working links
Step 9

Submit before the deadline

Submit on the workshop website and keep your reference number.

  • Verify with the Matric / Staff No. and email you registered with.
  • Paste the three links, complete the declarations and submit.
  • Keep your reference number. You may resubmit before the deadline; only the latest submission is reviewed.
  • Do not edit your files after the deadline.
Output: Submission reference numberSubmit your documents →
