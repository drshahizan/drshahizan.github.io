Phase 1 · Repository quality

Duplicate Detection

Identify repeated records consistently before screening and retain a transparent count for PRISMA reporting.

Current repository protection

The registration form checks DOI first, followed by an exact title-and-year match when a DOI is unavailable.

Review articles
01

Exact matches

Compare normalised DOI, article URL, and exact title. These signals provide the strongest duplicate evidence.

02

Probable matches

Review similar titles, matching publication years, journals, and first authors before making a decision.

03

Resolve

Keep the most complete record, merge useful metadata, and mark the other record as a confirmed duplicate.

04

Document

Record how many duplicates were removed so the PRISMA identification stage remains reproducible.

Decision guide

Compare before removing
