Common problems and practical corrections

ProblemWhy it mattersCorrection
SLR presented as a narrative summarySelection and synthesis cannot be evaluated.Document a transparent and reproducible review method.
RQ too broad or not alignedSearch and synthesis become unfocused.Map each RQ to concepts, eligibility and extraction fields.
Search strings not reportedSearch cannot be reproduced.Record database-specific strings, filters and search dates.
PRISMA diagram without a clear audit trailSelection numbers may be inconsistent.Maintain identification, deduplication and screening logs.
Counting studies instead of synthesising evidenceReview offers limited insight.Compare methods, contexts, contradictions and evidence strength.
Unsupported novelty claimsContribution is not established.Compare recent related reviews explicitly.
Unverified AI-generated citationsMay introduce fabricated sources.Verify every citation, DOI and extracted finding.

Pre-submission quick check

  • Can another researcher repeat the searches?
  • Are exclusion decisions and selection counts consistent?
  • Does every major conclusion trace to included evidence?
  • Are the review’s limitations and scope stated clearly?
  • Does the manuscript follow the target journal’s instructions?
