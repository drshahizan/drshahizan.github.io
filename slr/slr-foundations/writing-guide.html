Section-by-section writing roadmap

SectionWriting focusEvidence to use
IntroductionProblem, related reviews, gap, objectives and contribution.Review comparison matrix.
MethodsProtocol, searches, screening, appraisal, extraction and synthesis.Search logs, protocol, eligibility and PRISMA records.
ResultsStudy selection, characteristics and findings by RQ.Included studies and extraction matrix.
DiscussionInterpret findings, compare evidence and explain implications.Synthesis and appraisal limitations.
ConclusionAnswer the review objectives without unsupported claims.Findings and limitations.

Illustrative writing patterns (not text to copy blindly)

Research gap

“Previous reviews have examined [scope], but [specific issue] remains insufficiently synthesised in [context].”

Search methods

“We searched [databases] on [dates] using the documented strategy in [supplementary material].”

Synthesis

“Across the included studies, three recurring themes were identified: [themes]. The strength of evidence varied because [limitations].”

Report what was actually done. Never invent search counts, results, citations or quality scores.
