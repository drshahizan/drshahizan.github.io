What belongs in a review protocol?

ComponentWhat to document
Rationale and scopeWhy the review is necessary, target population, context and outcomes.
Research questionsClear answerable questions; PICOC or another suitable framework where helpful.
Search planDatabases, concepts, synonyms, search strings, dates and supplementary searching.
EligibilityInclusion and exclusion criteria with a rationale.
ScreeningHow records are deduplicated, screened and disagreements resolved.
AppraisalAppropriate risk-of-bias or quality assessment, if applicable.
Extraction and synthesisVariables, extraction process and intended synthesis methods.
ChangesProtocol amendments with dates and explanations.

Important distinction

PRISMA 2020 is primarily reporting guidance, not a protocol-development method. Where applicable, consult PRISMA-P for systematic review protocol reporting. Registration requirements depend on the review type and registry eligibility.
