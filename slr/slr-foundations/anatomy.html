Manuscript sections

SectionWhat to report
Title & AbstractReview focus, objectives, methods, key findings and conclusion.
IntroductionProblem context, rationale, gap and review objectives.
MethodsProtocol, RQs, databases, full search strategies, dates, eligibility, selection, appraisal and synthesis approach.
ResultsSearch and screening counts, study characteristics and evidence relevant to each RQ.
DiscussionInterpretation, contribution, implications, limitations and future directions.
ConclusionConcise answers and justified implications.
References & SupplementsCited sources, full search strings, forms, extracted data and reporting checklists as appropriate.

Follow the target journal's author instructions; no single manuscript template fits every discipline or review type.

Worked example

Topic: Explainable AI for IoT intrusion detection.

RQ: What XAI techniques are used, and how are effectiveness and interpretability evaluated?

Methods: State databases, exact searches, dates, screening decisions and synthesis method.

Results: Summarise included studies by XAI approach, dataset and evaluation criteria; do not invent study counts or citations.
