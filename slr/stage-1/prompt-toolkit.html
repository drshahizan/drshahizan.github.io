How to use a prompt
  1. Open the prompt for the step you are working on.
  2. Replace every bracketed field with your own information.
  3. Run it in an AI tool, then check suggestions against your research question and source records.
  4. Save only the relevant, verified Stage 1 output in your worksheet.

AI Output Verification Checklist

Before carrying an AI suggestion into your worksheet, verify it and keep the source evidence. Tick these items as you work; the checklist is not saved or submitted.

Open Stage 1 Search Log →

AI-assisted search activities should be described transparently in the methods when they materially affect how terms or records were identified.

Responsible-use instruction

Add this instruction to a prompt when you need stronger safeguards:

Use only the information I provide. Clearly separate facts from suggestions. Do not invent article titles, authors, DOIs, database results, search counts, or research gaps. If information is missing, ask me or mark it as missing. I will verify suggestions against original sources and database records.
