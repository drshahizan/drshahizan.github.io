STEP 01

Topic & Scope

Broad interest

Artificial intelligence in cybersecurity.

Focused direction

Explainable AI techniques used for intrusion detection in Internet of Things networks.

Working title

Explainable AI for Intrusion Detection in Internet of Things Networks: A Systematic Literature Review

Boundary decisions to make

  • Which forms of IoT or networked systems are in scope?
  • Which intrusion or anomaly detection tasks will be included?
  • Will the review include only empirical evaluations, or other study designs too?

Do not state that the topic is novel because an AI tool suggests it. Check prior reviews in suitable databases and record what you searched.

STEP 02

Research Questions

Example questions should follow from the scope and be answerable from the studies you plan to find.

  1. RQ1. What explainable AI techniques have been used for intrusion detection in Internet of Things networks?
  2. RQ2. How have detection effectiveness and explanation quality been evaluated in these studies?
Question focusConcepts to searchWhat the question does not decide yet
Methods / techniques (RQ1)Explainable AI · intrusion detection · IoTWhich studies will ultimately be included
Evaluation (RQ2)Detection evaluation · explainability evaluationWhether a specific metric is sufficient or comparable

For a computing topic, PICOC can help structure Population, Intervention, Comparison, Outcome and Context. Some elements—especially Comparison—may not be needed as search concepts. Select concepts based on the question and the available literature.

STEP 04

Database Selection

Choose sources because their scope fits the research question. These are example considerations, not a requirement to search every source.

SourcePossible role in this exampleDecision to document
ScopusMultidisciplinary citation-indexing databaseCoverage, subscription access and fields to search
Web of Science Core CollectionMultidisciplinary citation-indexing databaseAvailable indexes and overlap with other sources
IEEE XploreTechnical literature in computing, engineering and electronicsWhether its collection adds relevant coverage for this question
ScienceDirect / Springer Nature LinkPublisher platforms that may provide supplementary contentExplain why each platform is needed alongside cross-publisher indexes

A publisher platform and a citation-indexing database are not interchangeable. Record and justify each source separately; avoid assuming that platform searches provide comprehensive cross-publisher coverage.

STEP 05

Execute & Refine

  1. Choose one database and confirm its search interface and searchable fields.
  2. Adapt the generic string to that database using its official search guidance.
  3. Run the query and record the exact date, string, fields, filters and actual result count.
  4. Check whether known relevant seed papers are retrieved. Inspect missed or unexpected records and adjust one concept at a time.
  5. Record each change as a new search attempt; keep earlier versions for traceability.

STEP 06

Initial Article Set

  • Export records from each source in a supported format, such as RIS, BibTeX or CSV.
  • Keep original exports and note which database each file came from.
  • Import the records into Zotero and check for duplicates after combining sources.
  • Verify titles, authors, year and DOI against original records.

At this point, describe and organize the initial records. Full title/abstract screening, inclusion decisions and quality appraisal belong to later stages.
