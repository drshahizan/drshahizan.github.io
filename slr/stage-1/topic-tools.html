Stage 1 · Hands-on tool directory

Topic Discovery Tool Guides

Learn one tool at a time. Each guide starts with access and sign-in, explains features that help with SLR topic discovery, then walks you through a short practical exercise.

Open Topic & Scope worksheetStage 1 overview
How to use this directory: choose one tool, complete its exercise using your own research direction, verify any suggested article at its original scholarly record, then return to Topic & Scope and record only the concise output. Tool-generated suggestions are discovery leads, not evidence of a confirmed research gap.
