Scopus AI

Use Scopus AI to explore literature indexed in Scopus, where your institution provides access. Scopus AI can help turn a plain-language question into an exploration of indexed research, themes and related sources. Use it to orient your topic and identify records to inspect. It does not replace a documented, reproducible database search.

01 · Access and sign in

Get ready to use Scopus AI

Start from your university library website and open Scopus through its subscribed-resource link. Sign in using the institutional/SSO route if prompted. Once Scopus opens, look for the Scopus AI option. The feature may not be included in every institutional subscription; if it is unavailable, use the regular Scopus search or the Web of Science guide.

02 · Use it for SLR

Features that matter in this stage

Question-led exploration

Use a natural-language question to begin exploring a research area.

Themes and terminology

Inspect themes and related concepts surfaced from the indexed corpus.

Source-linked leads

Open the linked Scopus records and verify titles, abstracts and DOI.

Follow-up exploration

Ask a narrower follow-up question after reviewing the first result.

Keep the SLR boundary clear. This is a topic-discovery exercise. Your formal search strategy, search history and screening decisions belong in the relevant Stage 1 steps.

03 · Hands-on exercise

Try it with your own topic

  1. Open the UTM Library database portal (or your own institution’s library portal).
  2. Select Scopus and complete institutional sign-in if requested. Confirm that you can open article records.
  3. If available, open Scopus AI and enter a focused question about your topic.
  4. Review the themes and suggested records. Open at least two linked records in Scopus.
  5. Check each title, year, abstract and DOI; note useful terminology and questions.
  6. Record only a verified insight or seed paper in Topic & Scope. Document formal database queries later in the search log.

Copy-ready prompt / search instruction

Check before you continue: Access depends on institutional subscription and sign-in configuration. Scopus AI availability and features can vary. Do not treat an AI overview as the final search strategy or screening evidence.

04 · Take the result back

What should go into Topic & Scope?

A verified Scopus record, useful indexing terms and a theme or question to consider when refining scope.

Keep the output concise. Record the tool that suggested it, then verify any article or database claim at its source. Do not paste an unverified AI answer as evidence.
