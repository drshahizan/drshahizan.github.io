Stage 1 · Topic Discovery · Tool 03

Web of Science Research Assistant

Use Web of Science and its Research Assistant, if enabled by your institution, to explore indexed literature. The Research Assistant provides a conversational way to explore research within Web of Science. This guide focuses on topic discovery and record checking. Use the database’s standard search and saved search features for the formal, reproducible search process.

01 · Access and sign in

Get ready to use Web of Science Research Assistant

Open Web of Science through your university library’s subscribed-resource page. Choose institutional access or sign-in through your institution when prompted. After access is confirmed, check whether Research Assistant appears in your interface. If it does not, use the standard Web of Science search and follow the same verification steps.

02 · Use it for SLR

Features that matter in this stage

Conversational exploration

Ask a focused question to orient yourself in the indexed literature.

Topic and paper leads

Review suggested concepts and records as starting points for investigation.

Record verification

Open records in Web of Science to confirm bibliographic details and abstracts.

Follow-up questions

Narrow the exploration based on a theme, method or context you want to understand.

Keep the SLR boundary clear. This is a topic-discovery exercise. Your formal search strategy, search history and screening decisions belong in the relevant Stage 1 steps.

03 · Hands-on exercise

Try it with your own topic

  1. Enter Web of Science from the UTM Library portal or your institution’s library portal.
  2. Complete institutional sign-in, then confirm that you can open a record.
  3. If Research Assistant is available, ask one focused question about your topic.
  4. Select two suggested papers and open their Web of Science records. Verify title, year, abstract and DOI.
  5. Note one useful term or theme and one uncertainty that needs further searching.
  6. Return to Topic & Scope to record verified outputs; use the Stage 1 search log for formal searches.

Copy-ready prompt / search instruction

Check before you continue: Research Assistant availability depends on the Web of Science product and institutional subscription. If unavailable, continue with standard database search. Verify every suggested paper in its record.

04 · Take the result back

What should go into Topic & Scope?

A verified Web of Science paper, useful terms, and one theme or follow-up question.

Keep the output concise. Record the tool that suggested it, then verify any article or database claim at its source. Do not paste an unverified AI answer as evidence.
