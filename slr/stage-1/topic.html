Hands-on activity · 15–20 minutesUse your own research topic. By the end of this step, you will have a working title and an initial scope.
Narrow the topic

State the broad area and the focus you want to investigate.

Set boundaries

Define the context, issue and what is outside the review.

Check the focus

Make sure the focus is clear enough to develop research questions.

Worked example

Bidang luas: Artificial intelligence in cybersecurity
Fokus: Explainable AI for intrusion detection in Internet of Things networks
Tajuk kerja: Explainable AI Techniques for Intrusion Detection in IoT Networks

This example demonstrates how to narrow a topic. Replace it with your own research area.

Part A · Start with your research area

Write your broad research area or problem. Do not try to create a perfect title yet.

Part B · Choose a review focus

Complete these three components. A clear focus will help you develop research questions and keywords in the next steps.

Part C · Draft the title and set boundaries
Check before moving to Step 2
✓ The topic states a focus, not only a broad area.
✓ The scope names a clear context or domain.
✓ The topic can be turned into questions answerable from published studies.
✓ The boundaries are neither too broad nor too narrow.

If you are unsure, note what to discuss with your supervisor or a research partner. You can refine the scope after testing your search.
