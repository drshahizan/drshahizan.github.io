2. Develop research questions

Build 2–4 questions that are specific, answerable from published evidence and aligned with your topic. PICOC or another framework may help, but is not mandatory for every SLR.

Worked example

RQ1: What explainable AI techniques are used in IoT intrusion detection?
RQ2: How are detection effectiveness and interpretability evaluated?

Check: Each RQ must link to at least one search concept and later to extractable evidence.
