Key guidance

  • Start from where your included studies and the closest prior reviews were published — they show which journals accept this topic.
  • Confirm the journal explicitly accepts review articles (some only publish invited reviews).
  • Verify indexing directly in Scopus Sources and the Web of Science Master Journal List; do not rely on the journal’s own website.
  • Compare: aims and scope, article type and word limit, quartile, open-access options and APC, typical time to first decision, acceptance rate (if published).
  • Screen for predatory practices with Think. Check. Submit.: unsolicited invitations, guaranteed fast acceptance, unclear peer review, fake metrics.
