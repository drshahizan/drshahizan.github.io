SLR WRITING TOOLKIT · STAGE 4

Reference Checker for Included Studies

Catch wrong DOIs, fabricated references and retracted studies before a reviewer does. Especially important for your included studies, which are the evidence base of your review.

Paste your included studies or your reference list, one reference per line (up to 60). Each line can be a DOI, a DOI link or full reference text. The checker looks up each reference in Crossref (and DataCite for DOIs not in Crossref), compares the title, and checks Crossref for retractions, expressions of concern and corrections. Adapted for SLRs from the Polishing with AI reference checker ↗.

Your text is sent to the public Crossref and DataCite APIs to look up each reference. Nothing is stored by this site.

What the statuses mean

StatusMeaningWhat to do
Found · title matchesThe DOI exists and its title matches your text.Check authors, year and journal in your reference manager.
FoundThe DOI exists; you gave no title to compare.Confirm it is the study you meant.
Found · check titleThe DOI exists but points to a different title.The DOI may be wrong or belong to another paper. Correct it.
Possible match · verifyNo DOI given; Crossref returned a similar record.Open the record and confirm before adding its DOI.
DOI not foundNo Crossref or DataCite record exists for this DOI.Possible typo or fabricated reference. Find the original source.
No reliable matchNo DOI given and no similar record found.Search for the source manually; it may not exist or may not have a DOI.
Retracted Expression of concern CorrectionCrossref lists a notice updating this work.Read the notice. A retracted study should normally be excluded from your synthesis or discussed explicitly.

What this does not check

  • A matching DOI shows the paper exists; it does not show that the paper supports your claim. Read the source.
  • Retraction data come from Crossref and may lag behind the publisher. For important studies, also search the Retraction Watch Database ↗.
  • Books, reports and theses often have no DOI and may show “No reliable match” even when they are genuine.
  • The checker does not check reference style; use your reference manager for that.
