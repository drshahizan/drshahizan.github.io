Typical timings and what to do

  • With editor for more than 2–3 weeks: check the submission system, then send a short, polite enquiry.
  • Under review: times vary by field and journal; many journals publish their median time to first decision.
  • Revision requested: note the deadline and plan the response letter with Responding to Reviewers.
  • Rejected: read the reasons, revise, and choose the next journal from your shortlist. Never submit the same manuscript to two journals at once.
