SLR WRITING · STAGE 4

SLR Language Checker

Flag the wording problems that reviewers notice most in systematic reviews, before you submit.

Paste a paragraph or section of your SLR. The checker flags overclaiming, unsupported novelty, vague or non-reproducible methods, generic AI-sounding phrasing, percentages without denominators, mixed terminology for included studies and possible study-by-study summaries. It runs entirely in your browser; nothing is stored or sent. It is a prompt to review your text, not a judgement: read each flag and decide.
