Key guidance

  • Tick an item only when the manuscript actually reports it. Enter the section or page number so the journal’s checklist can be completed quickly.
  • Items 12, 14, 15, 21 and 22 are often not applicable to qualitative syntheses — say so explicitly with a reason rather than leaving them blank.
  • Many journals require the completed PRISMA checklist as a supplementary file at submission.
  • Consider relevant extensions: PRISMA-S (search reporting), PRISMA-ScR (scoping reviews) or discipline-specific guidance.

PRISMA 2020 main checklist (27 items)

Counted as complete when an item is marked Reported or Not applicable. Item wording is abridged — use the official checklist for sub-items (e.g. 10a/10b, 13a–f, 20a–d, 23a–d, 24a–c).

5Eligibility criteriaInclusion and exclusion criteria and how studies were grouped for the syntheses.
