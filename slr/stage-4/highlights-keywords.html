Highlights and Keywords Checker

Check your highlights, keywords and title against the limits of your target journal before you submit.

Many journals ask for highlights (short bullet points shown with the article) and a limited number of keywords. Elsevier journals, for example, usually ask for 3–5 highlights of up to 85 characters each, including spaces. Limits differ between journals, so set them from your target journal's guide for authors. The check runs in your browser and is saved only there.

        Writing good SLR highlights

        • Each highlight states one finding or contribution, not a section summary (“The results are discussed” says nothing).
        • Give the evidence base once: “Synthesises 26 studies published 2015–2025.”
        • Use the same hedging as the article: “suggests”, “is associated with”. Never stronger than the abstract.
        • Keywords should add search terms that are not already in the title, including synonyms readers search for.
