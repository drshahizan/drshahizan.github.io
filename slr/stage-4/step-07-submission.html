Key guidance

  • Cover letter: address the editor by name, state the article type and title, explain the contribution and fit with the journal’s scope in one paragraph, and confirm originality and declarations.
  • Highlights (if required): 3–5 bullets, each about 85 characters, stating findings rather than activities.
  • Suggested reviewers: active researchers in the topic, from different institutions and countries, with no recent collaboration with any author.
  • Final read-through on the PDF generated by the submission system: check figures, tables, special characters and line numbers.
  • Keep a submission record: journal, date, manuscript ID, version files and correspondence.
