AI Disclosure Generator for SLRs

Describe how you used AI in your review. You get a disclosure statement and the sentences to report AI use in your Methods section.

In a systematic review, AI may be used not only for language editing but also in the methods: screening, extraction, appraisal or synthesis. Methodological use must be reported in the Methods section (PRISMA 2020 item 8 asks for automation tools used in selection), as well as in the AI disclosure statement. This generator runs entirely in your browser; nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.

Before you use the text

  • Check the journal’s own Guide for Authors: requirements differ between journals of the same publisher.
  • AI tools cannot be listed as authors. The authors remain responsible for every decision and every word.
  • Keep a log of the prompts, tool versions and dates you used, in case reviewers ask.
  • Do not upload unpublished manuscripts or copyrighted full texts to AI tools unless the licence and the tool’s data policy allow it.
