Key guidance

  • Insert citations only through a reference manager, then switch to the target journal’s style (CSL/EndNote style).
  • Check DOI, author names, year, volume and pages for every included study — these are the evidence base of your review.
  • Search for retractions and expressions of concern (e.g. Retraction Watch database, Scite, Crossref metadata).
  • Distinguish clearly between included studies and background references; many reviews mark included studies with an ID or asterisk.
  • Never cite a reference suggested by AI without opening and reading the original source.
