Key guidance

  • Download the journal’s guide for authors and turn it into a checklist: article type, word limit, abstract format, headings, reference style, figure format, supplementary files.
  • Data availability: share the search strings, screening log, extraction and appraisal data (e.g. OSF, Zenodo, Mendeley Data) where permitted.
  • AI-use statement: most publishers require disclosure of generative-AI assistance in writing; AI tools cannot be listed as authors.
  • Registration: report the protocol registration (PROSPERO, OSF) or state that the review was not registered.
  • Make sure every author has an ORCID iD and has agreed on authorship order and CRediT roles.
Example AI disclosure (adapt to the journal’s wording):

“During the preparation of this work the authors used [tool, version] to [purpose, e.g. improve readability of selected paragraphs]. After using this tool, the authors reviewed and edited the content as needed and take full responsibility for the content of the publication.”
