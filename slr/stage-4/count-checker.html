Do your PRISMA diagram, article text and files report the same numbers? Enter what each one says and see every mismatch before a reviewer does.

The same numbers appear in your PRISMA diagram, Abstract, Methods, Results, characteristics table, quality assessment Excel file and reference list. Reviewers check that they agree.
