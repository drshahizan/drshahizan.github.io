Stage 3 guided workspace

Stage 2 produced your final included-study set, PRISMA counts, appraisal results and extraction matrix. In Stage 3, turn that evidence into a synthesis that answers each research question, then draft the manuscript around it.

Follow the seven worksheets in order. They save in this browser; the Portfolio gathers your reviewed outputs.

  1. Prepare the Evidence Base — Confirm the final included-study set and make the extraction matrix analysis-ready.
  2. Choose the Synthesis Approach — Select and justify a synthesis method that fits your RQs and the type of evidence you have.
  3. Descriptive Mapping — Describe the evidence base: when, where, how and by whom the included studies were produced.
  4. Thematic Synthesis by RQ — Move from extracted findings to codes, themes and an answer for each research question.
  5. Research Gaps and Agenda — Derive evidence-based research gaps and turn them into a concrete future research agenda.
  6. Evidence Tables and Figures — Design the core tables and figures that make your synthesis transparent and easy to review.
  7. Draft the Manuscript — Write the first full draft section by section, starting with the parts you already have evidence for.
  8. Review and export the Stage 3 Portfolio

Tool guides

Use the separate tool pages for setup and demonstrations. Return to the worksheets to record outputs.

  • VOSviewer — keyword and co-authorship maps for descriptive results.
  • NVivo — code extracted findings and build themes.
  • ATLAS.ti — alternative qualitative coding environment.
  • Gemini Notebook — query selected full texts with citations to verify.
  • diagrams.net — draw conceptual frameworks and PRISMA figures.
  • Zotero — insert and manage citations while drafting.

TAKE-HOME TASK · BEFORE STAGE 4

Complete the full manuscript draft

Use the synthesis structure practised in Stage 3 to complete a full draft before Stage 4.

  • Finish thematic synthesis for every RQ using all included studies.
  • Complete the tables and figures, including a final PRISMA flow diagram.
  • Draft all manuscript sections and the structured abstract.
  • Bring the full draft, PRISMA checklist (draft), reference library and target journal ideas to Stage 4.
Stage 4 readiness check: A colleague can read your draft and find, for every result, the included studies that support it.
