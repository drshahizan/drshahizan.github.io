Crafting the Systematic Literature Review Manuscript

Transform extracted evidence into a coherent scholarly manuscript that answers the review questions.

WRITE
LEARN→DEMONSTRATION→HANDS-ON→DELIVERABLE

What participants will do

  • Synthesize findings using appropriate narrative or thematic approaches
  • Identify patterns, gaps and research implications
  • Design evidence tables and figures
  • Draft Introduction and Method
  • Develop Results and Discussion around the research questions
  • Maintain evidence-based academic writing

Methodology first, tools second

The systematic review method is the backbone of this stage. AI and digital tools are introduced only where they support a defined methodological task. Participants remain responsible for research decisions, verification and scholarly judgement.
