Literature Discovery and Search Strategy

Define a focused review direction and build a reproducible literature search.

DISCOVER
LEARN→DEMONSTRATION→HANDS-ON→DELIVERABLE

What participants will do

  • Refine the research topic and research questions
  • Develop keywords, synonyms and Boolean search strings
  • Select appropriate scholarly databases
  • Run and document searches
  • Use digital discovery and visualization tools where they add value

Methodology first, tools second

The systematic review method is the backbone of this stage. AI and digital tools are introduced only where they support a defined methodological task. Participants remain responsible for research decisions, verification and scholarly judgement.
