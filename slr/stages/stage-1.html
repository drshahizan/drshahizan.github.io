STAGE 1 → STAGE 2

Prepare your Stage 2 handoff

Before screening begins, keep the decision record and the original database exports together. The portfolio is a summary; it does not replace the source files.

Bring these documents
  • Stage 1 portfolio: scope, research questions and draft eligibility criteria
  • Search log with the exact queries, run dates, filters and result counts
  • Original database exports (for example, RIS or BibTeX), kept unchanged
Check before Stage 2
  • Exported records include titles, abstracts, years, DOI and source database where available
  • Save a PDF or JSON backup of the Stage 1 portfolio
  • Keep one untouched copy of the exports for reconciliation
