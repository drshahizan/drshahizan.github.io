Stage 4 · 19 November 2026

Manuscript Refinement and Publication Readiness

Strengthen the manuscript, check reporting quality and prepare a realistic path towards journal submission.

REFINE
LEARN→DEMONSTRATION→HANDS-ON→DELIVERABLE

What participants will do

  • Review argument, structure and evidence alignment
  • Check reporting completeness and references
  • Improve clarity, consistency and academic presentation
  • Match the manuscript to suitable journals
  • Check journal scope and author requirements
  • Prepare the manuscript for final submission steps

Methodology first, tools second

The systematic review method is the backbone of this stage. AI and digital tools are introduced only where they support a defined methodological task. Participants remain responsible for research decisions, verification and scholarly judgement.
