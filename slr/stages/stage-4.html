Stage 4 guided workspace

Stage 3 produced a full manuscript draft. In Stage 4, strengthen its argument and reporting, verify every reference, choose a suitable journal and assemble a complete submission package.

Follow the seven worksheets in order. They save in this browser; the Portfolio gathers your reviewed outputs.

  1. Structure and Argument — Check that the manuscript answers its research questions and makes a clear contribution.
  2. PRISMA 2020 Compliance — Check the manuscript against all 27 PRISMA 2020 items and the abstract checklist, and record page locations.
  3. References and Citations — Make every citation accurate, consistent, traceable and appropriate.
  4. Language and Presentation — Improve clarity, consistency and academic tone while keeping the meaning and your own voice.
  5. Journal Matching — Shortlist suitable, legitimate journals and choose a realistic first target.
  6. Author Guidelines and Declarations — Align the manuscript with the target journal’s guidelines and prepare the required declarations.
  7. Submission Package — Assemble every file the submission system will ask for and run a final readiness check.
  8. Review and export the Stage 4 Portfolio

Tool guides

Use the separate tool pages for setup and demonstrations. Return to the worksheets to record outputs.

  • Zotero — switch citation styles and clean the reference library.
  • Scite — check how references are cited and flag retractions.
  • Grammarly — language editing — accept changes one by one.
  • Paperpal — academic language and submission checks.
  • Scopus — verify journal indexing and find where similar reviews are published.
  • ORCID — author identifiers required by most journals.

TAKE-HOME TASK · AFTER THE WORKSHOP

Move from a refined draft to submission

Complete the revisions identified in Stage 4 and prepare the final submission files.

  • Complete the revision plan and re-check the RQ alignment map.
  • Finalise the PRISMA 2020 checklist with page numbers for every item.
  • Confirm the target journal’s current guidelines and prepare all declarations.
  • Obtain co-author approval, assemble the submission package and keep a submission record.
