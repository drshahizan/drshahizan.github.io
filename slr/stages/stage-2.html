TAKE-HOME TASK · BEFORE STAGE 3

Complete your review across the full set of studies

The workshop activities and shared practice pack help you learn the process. Before Stage 3, apply the confirmed protocol to your complete review set and bring verified outputs for synthesis.

  • Finish title/abstract and full-text screening; record a reason for each full-text exclusion.
  • Reconcile the PRISMA counts with your screening records.
  • Complete quality appraisal and data extraction for every included study.
  • Save your final included-study list, evidence-based extraction matrix and a backup of your review files.
Ready for Stage 3? You can identify which studies were included, explain the exclusions, and trace the evidence for each research question to its source.
