Systematic Review and Evidence Synthesis

Move from retrieved records to a transparent, defensible final study set and extraction framework.

REVIEW
LEARN→DEMONSTRATION→HANDS-ON→DELIVERABLE

What participants will do

  • Establish the review protocol and eligibility criteria
  • Remove duplicates and document the process
  • Conduct title, abstract and full-text screening
  • Track selection using PRISMA
  • Assess study quality
  • Design and begin the data extraction matrix

Methodology first, tools second

The systematic review method is the backbone of this stage. AI and digital tools are introduced only where they support a defined methodological task. Participants remain responsible for research decisions, verification and scholarly judgement.
