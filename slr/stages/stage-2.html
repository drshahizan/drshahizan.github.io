Stage 2 · REVIEW

Systematic Review and Evidence Synthesis

Move from retrieved records to a transparent final study set and a usable evidence matrix.

Stage 1 produced your protocol draft, database exports and search log. In Stage 2, apply the planned rules consistently, document every decision and prepare evidence for synthesis.

LEARN · PILOT · SCREEN · APPRAISE · EXTRACT
LEARN→DEMONSTRATION→HANDS-ON→DELIVERABLE

Stage 2 workflow

  1. Prepare the review pack. Confirm the protocol version, eligibility rules, search exports and source counts from Stage 1.
  2. Deduplicate records. Combine exports, review possible matches and retain a record of counts.
  3. Pilot the criteria. Two reviewers independently assess a small shared sample; discuss disagreements and clarify rules before full screening.
  4. Screen titles and abstracts. Record include, exclude or uncertain decisions. Retrieve full texts for potentially eligible records.
  5. Screen full texts. Apply the same criteria and assign a specific primary exclusion reason to each excluded report.
  6. Reconcile counts. Check that identification, deduplication, screening and inclusion numbers add up in the flow diagram.
  7. Appraise and extract. Pilot the appraisal checklist and extraction form, then apply them consistently to the included evidence.
  8. Prepare the synthesis. Group extracted findings against each research question; flag patterns, contradictions and evidence gaps for later writing.
!
Keep human decisions auditable

Screening and appraisal tools can organise work and flag possible matches. Reviewers make, verify and document the final decisions.
