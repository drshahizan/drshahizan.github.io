TAKE-HOME TASK · COMPLETE BEFORE STAGE 3

Continue from the pilot to your full review

Use the decisions and templates practised in Stage 2 across your complete set of records and included studies. The portfolio summarizes your work; retain the full record-level dataset in your selected review system.

  • Complete title/abstract and full-text decisions, including reasons for exclusion.
  • Check that PRISMA counts reconcile with the screening log.
  • Finish quality appraisal and evidence-based extraction for every included study.
  • Bring the final included-study list, PRISMA counts, appraisal results and extraction matrix to Stage 3.
Stage 3 readiness check: Every synthesis claim should be traceable to an included study and a recorded evidence location, such as a page number or section.
