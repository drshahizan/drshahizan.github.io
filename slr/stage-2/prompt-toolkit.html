How to use the prompts
  1. Open the relevant step and tool guide (for example Zotero, Rayyan, Covidence, Gemini or ChatGPT).
  2. Replace every bracketed field; share only material you are permitted to process.
  3. Ask for evidence and uncertainty. Verify against the original record, protocol or full text.
  4. Make and record the human decision in your review system. Copy only a concise, verified summary to the worksheet.

AI Output Verification Checklist

AI output is a working aid. Verify every decision and factual statement against protocol, reviewer logs, full text and source records. Tick items as you work; this checklist is not saved.

Do not treat an AI response as a reviewer vote, a source record, or a substitute for the audit trail.

Responsible-use instruction

Add this to a prompt when you need stronger safeguards:

Use only the information I provide. Separate direct evidence from suggestions. Do not invent citations, records, counts, quotations or page numbers. If information is missing, state “not reported” or ask me. Do not make final review decisions; a human reviewer will verify and record them.
