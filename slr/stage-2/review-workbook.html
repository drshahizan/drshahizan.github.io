STAGE 2 · HANDS-ON WORKBOOK

Screen, Appraise and Extract

Use this workbook to rehearse the Stage 2 decisions and reconcile counts. The form saves in this browser. Export a copy before changing devices or clearing browser data.

← Stage 2 overview

01 · Before screening

Protocol check

Carry forward the approved version from Stage 1. Any change should be dated, justified and recorded before applying it to the remaining studies.

Calibrate on a small shared sample first. Discuss disagreements, clarify ambiguous rules, then begin full screening.

02 · Pilot exercise

Screening decision log

Enter a small pilot batch. This is a practice record; keep the full dataset and decisions in your selected review system.

Record / DOIDecisionReason or note

03 · Selection audit trail

PRISMA count reconciliation

Enter actual counts from your exports and screening. The workbook calculates basic checkpoints so you can spot mismatches before preparing the flow diagram.

The workbook checks arithmetic only. Confirm category definitions and reporting against your protocol and PRISMA flow diagram.

04 · Evidence appraisal

Pilot quality appraisal and extraction

Choose criteria that fit the included study designs and research questions. Pilot the form on varied studies; record evidence and rationale instead of relying on an unexplained score.

Do not use a health-specific risk-of-bias checklist by default for computing/software engineering studies. Match appraisal criteria to the review question and study design.
