STAGE 2 · INTERACTIVE TOOL

PRISMA Flow Diagram Builder

Enter verified counts to build a review flow diagram. The builder checks the arithmetic and creates a two-column flow diagram like the example, including source names and counts.

Use verified review records. This builder uses the database/register pathway shown in the example. It checks count consistency but cannot validate your screening decisions. If your review also identifies studies through other methods, use the matching official PRISMA template. Entries stay in this browser.

02 · LIVE PREVIEW

Flow diagram

Enter your verified counts to update the diagram.

PRISMA 2020-style draft based on the database/register pathway. Verify every count against the search log and screening record before reporting.

Before you export

  1. Reconcile source totals and duplicate removals with your database exports.
  2. Check that screening exclusions, reports not retrieved and full-text exclusion reasons match your decision log.
  3. Confirm the final number of included studies. Keep study counts separate from report counts.
  4. Export the SVG for publication-quality editing, PNG for slides, or print to PDF. Keep a CSV/JSON backup of the entered data.

For publication, compare this draft with the official PRISMA 2020 flow diagram templates. This page is for new reviews using databases and registers.
