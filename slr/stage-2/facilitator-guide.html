Expected deduplication

Start with 23 imported records. Retain one record from each duplicate pair: P21 duplicates P01; P22 duplicates P11; P23 duplicates P04. Remove three duplicate instances, leaving 20 unique records for title/abstract screening. These are designed duplicates within the exercise.

Title and abstract screening key

DecisionRecord IDsRationale
IncludeP01, P02, P03, P08, P11, P18Abstracts indicate a primary study in IoT intrusion detection using ML and feature selection/extraction. P18 is a deliberate full-text trap: abstract-stage inclusion is reasonable.
ExcludeP04–P07, P09–P10, P12–P17Use the primary exclusion reason in the downloadable key: wrong context, method, outcome, publication type, date, non-empirical design or non-separable IoT results.
UncertainP19, P20Abstracts do not establish context/method sufficiently; retrieve and inspect full-text simulation notes.

Reviewers may use different wording for reasons. Check whether the decision follows the protocol and whether the reason is explicit and reproducible.

Full-text decisions

  • Include: P01, P02, P03, P08, P11 and P20.
  • Exclude: P18 because no feature-selection/extraction method is evaluated; P19 because the actual study context is wired industrial control, not IoT.

P18 demonstrates that a record may reasonably pass abstract screening but fail full-text assessment when the full method is clearer. P19 demonstrates why an uncertain abstract should proceed to full text instead of being excluded based on a guess.

Suggested debrief questions

  1. Which inclusion criterion caused the most disagreement?
  2. Did anyone exclude P19 too early? What information was missing from its abstract?
  3. Why can P18 pass abstract screening but fail full-text assessment?
  4. How did the group decide that P21–P23 were duplicate records rather than separate studies?
  5. Can every full-text exclusion be assigned one primary, protocol-linked reason?
  6. What changes should be recorded in the protocol before screening the remaining records?
