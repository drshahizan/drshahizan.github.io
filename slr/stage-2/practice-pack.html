Exercise scenario and protocol

Practise a review question about feature selection or feature extraction methods used in machine-learning intrusion detection for Internet of Things (IoT) environments.

Include when all apply

  • Peer-reviewed journal or conference primary study.
  • Published from 2019 to 2025 inclusive.
  • Studies IoT devices, networks or IoT traffic.
  • Uses machine learning for intrusion/attack detection.
  • Evaluates feature selection, ranking or extraction and reports relevant results.

Exclude when any apply

  • Outside the date range, or not a primary empirical paper.
  • Wrong context, such as cloud-only, Android-only or non-IoT industrial systems.
  • Not intrusion/attack detection, or no machine learning.
  • No evaluated feature selection/extraction method.
  • IoT results cannot be separated or extracted.

Apply these as the exercise protocol. In a real review, criteria must be justified by the review question and documented in the protocol.

Step-by-step activity

1

Inspect and preserve the source set

Download the CSV. Note the number of imported records and keep an untouched copy before changing anything.

2

Find duplicates before screening

Compare title, authors, year and abstract. Record duplicate pairs; retain one copy of each unique record. Do not confuse a duplicate database record with a related but distinct study.

3

Calibrate independently

Each reviewer screens the same small batch without discussing decisions first. Mark Include, Exclude or Uncertain and write the criterion that supports each decision.

4

Compare and clarify

Compare decisions, especially disagreements and uncertain records. Explain which rule caused the difference. Clarify the rule before completing the rest of the set.

5

Complete title and abstract screening

Record one primary reason for every exclusion. Use Uncertain when the abstract does not provide enough information; do not infer missing details.

6

Release and assess full-text notes

After abstract decisions are recorded, the facilitator may provide the full-text simulation notes. Reassess the candidate reports and record the final decision and reason.

7

Reconcile the flow counts

Enter the counts in the Stage 2 guided workspace. Explain any difference and verify that each record is accounted for.

8

Debrief and transfer

Discuss ambiguous criteria, then repeat the workflow with your own Stage 1 exports. Keep the actual review decisions in the selected review system and maintain a backup.
