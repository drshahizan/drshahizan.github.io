SLR TOOL DIRECTORY

Tools for Producing an SLR Article

Find tools by the work you need to do—from discovering literature and screening evidence to writing and preparing a manuscript for publication.

Start with the method and required output for each stage. Then choose a tool that supports the task, verify its results, and record decisions in the SLR workspace.

Method first. Tools support the work.

Tools can help with searching, organising, analysing and communicating evidence. They do not replace review decisions or source verification.

  • Learn the workflow
  • Choose tools for a defined task
  • Verify and document outputs

BROWSE BY SLR STAGE

Choose tools for your current task

The guides linked as “In this website” provide the local learning steps. BDC collections offer a broader catalogue for additional tools; not every tool in those collections is needed for every SLR.

01

Discover

Define, discover & search

Develop a focused review direction, build and test search terms, select suitable databases, and organise the initial records.

  • Topic, research questions, keyword matrix and Boolean strategy
  • Database search, search log and initial article set
  • Reference management and citation discovery
Stage output: focused questions, tested search strategy, documented search plan and verified initial article set.
Stage 1 workspace →Tool guides →BDC: Discovery & Databases ↗BDC: Paper Visualisation ↗

In this website: Gemini, Scopus AI, Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, ResearchRabbit, Publish or Perish and Zotero guides.

02

Review

Select & assess evidence

Manage records, remove duplicates, apply eligibility criteria, assess study quality and prepare a consistent extraction framework.

  • Deduplication and study management
  • Title, abstract and full-text screening
  • Quality assessment and evidence extraction
Stage output: traceable study-selection decisions, quality records and an extraction framework.
Duplicate detection →Screening →Quality assessment →Study management →BDC: Review & Evidence Analysis ↗BDC: Methods & Productivity ↗
03

Write

Synthesise & draft the article

Turn extracted evidence into structured findings, tables, figures and a manuscript aligned with the review questions.

  • Compare studies and synthesise patterns
  • Build evidence tables and visual summaries
  • Draft sections from verified evidence and citations
Stage output: evidence-linked synthesis and a complete working SLR manuscript.
Synthesis →Manuscript writing →Stage 3 materials →BDC: Writing, Citation & Publishing ↗BDC: Paper Visualisation ↗
04

Refine

Check & prepare for publication

Review reporting completeness, references, clarity, journal fit and submission requirements before preparing a final manuscript.

  • Check reporting and reference accuracy
  • Improve clarity and consistency
  • Compare journal scope and author instructions
Stage output: revised manuscript, journal shortlist with rationale, and submission-readiness checklist.
Stage 4 overview →Stage 4 materials →BDC: Writing, Citation & Publishing ↗BDC: Methods & Productivity ↗
