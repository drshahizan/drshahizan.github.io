Tools for Producing an SLR Article

Explore a locally hosted catalogue of tools for literature discovery, evidence review, synthesis, writing and publication readiness.

Choose a tool for a defined task, practise on a small example, verify its output, and record decisions in the relevant SLR worksheet.

Method first. Tools support the work.

Tools can help search, organise, analyse and communicate evidence. They do not replace review decisions or source verification.

Find a tool for your task

The directory includes all tools currently listed in the five reference collections and Scopus AI retained for Stage 1. Every description and learning page is hosted in this SLR website. Product websites are linked only for access.

How to use this directory

1 · Start with the task

Identify the method or output you need before choosing a tool.

2 · Practise on a small example

Use a seed article or sample data and follow the tool’s dedicated guide.

3 · Verify and document

Check outputs against original sources and record the tool, input and decision.

Access and responsible use: Sign-in, institutional access, features and pricing vary by provider and may change. Follow UTM access requirements. Do not upload unpublished manuscripts, restricted full texts or personal data unless the provider and your institution permit it. Verify AI-suggested claims and references against original sources.
