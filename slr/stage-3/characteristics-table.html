Study Characteristics Table Builder

Load your spreadsheet, pick the columns, and get a formatted characteristics table for Word or LaTeX plus distribution charts for your Results section.

Turn your data extraction or quality assessment spreadsheet into a study characteristics table and distribution charts (studies by year, country, design or any other column). Everything runs in your browser; your file is not uploaded or stored.

1. Load your data

Practice data are synthetic. Header row: if your sheet has a title above the column names, set the row that holds the column names.

2. Choose rows and columns

Columns in the table (tick to include; the order follows your file)

3. Study characteristics table

4. Distribution charts

Report the denominator in the caption, e.g. “Figure 3. Included studies by country (n = 24)”. When one study has several values, counts can add up to more than n; say so in the caption.

Good practice

  • Keep the characteristics table descriptive: study ID, year, country or setting, design, sample, and the variables your RQs need. Interpretation belongs in the synthesis.
  • Use the same study IDs as your PRISMA diagram, quality assessment file and reference list.
  • Long tables usually go to a supplementary file; keep a shorter summary table in the article.
