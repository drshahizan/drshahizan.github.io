Common mistakes

  • A figure with no interpretation in the text (“Figure 3 shows the number of studies by country.”). Say what the pattern means.
  • Pie charts with many slices, and 3D charts.
  • Bibliometric maps used as decoration, unrelated to any RQ.
  • Counts that differ between figures, tables and text.
  • Colour as the only way to tell categories apart; add labels or patterns for greyscale printing.
  • Low resolution: most journals require 300 dpi or vector formats (PDF, SVG, EPS).
