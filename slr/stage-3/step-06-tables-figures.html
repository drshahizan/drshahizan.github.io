Key guidance

Table / figurePurposeNotes
PRISMA 2020 flow diagramStudy selection (Item 16)Required. Counts must match the text and abstract.
Table of included studiesStudy characteristics (Item 17)ID, citation, year, country, design, sample, intervention, outcomes.
Quality appraisal tableRisk of bias / quality (Item 18)Criterion-level judgements per study, plus overall rating.
Theme summary tableResults of syntheses (Item 20)Theme, definition, supporting studies, strength of evidence.
Descriptive chartsOverview of the evidence baseYear trend, designs, domains — only when they add insight.
Conceptual framework / mapSynthesis contributionRelationships between themes; often the paper’s most cited figure.
  • Tables carry detail; the text interprets. Do not repeat every table value in the prose.
  • Each table/figure needs a self-explanatory caption, defined abbreviations and the source of data.
  • Export figures at the resolution the journal requires (often 300 dpi or vector) and avoid colour-only coding.
  • Very long tables (e.g. all included studies) can go to a supplementary file — check the journal’s policy.
