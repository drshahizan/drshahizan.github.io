How to use the prompts
  1. Open the matching step and gather your verified inputs (matrix, themes, draft text).
  2. Replace every bracketed field; share only material you are permitted to process.
  3. Ask for evidence and uncertainty; compare the output with your sources.
  4. Edit the output in your own words and record what you used in the AI-assistance log.

Responsible-use instruction

Add this to a prompt when you need stronger safeguards:

Use only the information I provide. Separate direct evidence from suggestions. Do not invent citations, study IDs, counts, quotations or page numbers. If information is missing, state “not reported” or ask me. Do not make final analytical or authorship decisions; I will verify and decide.
