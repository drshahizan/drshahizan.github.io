SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA

Purpose

Practise moving from an extraction matrix to codes, themes, a synthesised results paragraph and evidence-based research gaps — using a small, shared set of six fictional studies. Everyone works on the same data, so the group can compare themes and discuss differences in interpretation.

Practice review question

RQ (practice): How does AI-generated feedback influence novice programmers’ performance and help-seeking behaviour, and what limits the current evidence?

Files

  • Synthesis practice matrix (CSV) — pre-filled extraction for six synthetic studies SYN-01 to SYN-06.
  • Full synthetic texts for SYN-01, SYN-02 and SYN-03 (from Stage 2).
  • Theme coding matrix (blank)

Activity (45–60 minutes)

  1. Individually (15 min): read the “Key findings” and “Limitations” columns. Write 8–12 short codes with study IDs.
  2. In pairs (15 min): merge your codes and group them into 3–4 descriptive themes. Write a one-sentence definition for each theme.
  3. In pairs (10 min): for each theme, list supporting and contradicting studies and judge the strength of evidence using the “Quality” column.
  4. Individually (10 min): write one synthesised results paragraph for your strongest theme and two evidence-based research gaps.
  5. Whole group (10 min): compare themes. Where did pairs disagree, and why?

Your practice notes

Saved locally as you type

Debrief questions

  • Did your paragraph compare studies, or describe them one by one?
  • Which claims would change if you excluded the lowest-quality studies?
  • Is “students value AI feedback” a finding about learning, or about perception? How should it be worded?

Facilitators: a suggested theme structure is in practice-pack/facilitator-theme-key.csv. See the facilitator notes.
