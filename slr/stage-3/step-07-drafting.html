Key guidance

Writing orderWhat to write
1 · MethodAdapt from your protocol and Stage 1–2 portfolios; past tense; enough detail to reproduce the review.
2 · ResultsSelection → descriptive overview → one sub-section per RQ, organised by theme.
3 · DiscussionPrincipal findings → comparison with prior reviews → implications → limitations → agenda.
4 · IntroductionContext → problem → what existing reviews cover → gap → aim and RQs.
5 · ConclusionAnswer the RQs concisely; no new evidence.
6 · Title & AbstractWritten last; follow PRISMA 2020 for Abstracts.
  • Recommended writing order is shown above: begin with sections that are already documented in your portfolios.
  • Use one paragraph per idea: topic sentence (the synthesised claim) → evidence across studies with citations → interpretation.
  • Keep a claim–evidence audit: for each key claim, list the supporting study IDs and evidence location.
  • If you use AI writing support, keep a log of what was generated or edited and check your target journal’s AI policy.
