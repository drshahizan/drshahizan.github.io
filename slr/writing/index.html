SLR WRITING MODULES

SLR Writing Modules

Ten short modules on turning a systematic review into a publishable article. Each module has learning outcomes, key points, an example and a checklist you can tick.

W1Ethics and Responsible AI in SLRsAccountability, confidentiality and transparency when AI touches your review.W2What Journals Expect from an SLRContribution, reproducibility and reporting standards that editors check first.W3AI Tools Across the SLR WorkflowWhich tools help at each stage, and what each one should and should not do.W4Writing an SLR Section by SectionWhat each section of an SLR must contain, from title to conclusion.W5Language and Style for SLRsTense, hedging, numbers and terminology conventions specific to reviews.W6From Summary to SynthesisBuilding an argument and a clear contribution from many studies.W7References and Citation Integrity in SLRsManaging included studies and background references accurately.W8Peer Review and Responding for SLRsAnticipating and answering what reviewers ask about systematic reviews.W9Limits and Risks of AI in SLRsWhere AI goes wrong in reviews, and how to guard against it.W10A Practical Workflow: From Quality Assessment to SubmissionA step-by-step route from your appraised studies to a submitted article.

How the modules fit with the rest of the site

  • Modules explain why and how: what an SLR article needs and what reviewers expect.
  • Stage 3 and Stage 4 worksheets are where you do the work and record your outputs.
  • The SLR Writing Toolkit provides the tools: phrasebank, examples, checkers, generators and checklists.
  • AI policies of major publishers: where and how to disclose AI use.
