Writing modules

Nine short modules. Each has learning outcomes, key points, an example and a checklist you can tick. They explain why and how; the Stage 3 and 4 worksheets are where you do the work.

W1Responsible AI in SLRs: Ethics, Risks and SafeguardsAccountability, confidentiality, failure modes and safeguards when AI touches your review.W2What Journals Expect from an SLRContribution, reproducibility and reporting standards that editors check first.W3AI Tools Across the SLR WorkflowWhich tools help at each stage, and what each one should and should not do.W4Writing an SLR Section by SectionWhat each section of an SLR must contain, from title to conclusion.W5Language and Style for SLRsTense, hedging, numbers and terminology conventions specific to reviews.W6From Summary to SynthesisBuilding an argument and a clear contribution from many studies.W7References and Citation Integrity in SLRsManaging included studies and background references accurately.W8Peer Review and Responding for SLRsAnticipating and answering what reviewers ask about systematic reviews.W9A Practical Workflow: From Quality Assessment to SubmissionA step-by-step route from your appraised studies to a submitted article.
