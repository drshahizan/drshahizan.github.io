SLR WRITING MODULES · W10

A Practical Workflow: From Quality Assessment to Submission

A step-by-step route from your appraised studies to a submitted article.

Learning outcomes

  • Plan the writing stage after quality assessment
  • Know which tool or worksheet to use at each step
  • Estimate realistic time for each step

Key points

Workflow

  • 1. Finalise the evidence base (1–2 days): included studies, PRISMA counts, QA file → After Quality Assessment.
  • 2. Extract and code (3–7 days) → Stage 2 · Extraction.
  • 3. Synthesise by research question (1–2 weeks) → Stage 3 · Thematic synthesis.
  • 4. Build tables and figures (2–4 days) → Figure gallery.
  • 5. Draft Methods, Results, Discussion, Introduction, abstract (2–3 weeks) → W4, Phrasebank.
  • 6. Refine (1 week): argument, language, references, PRISMA → Stage 4.
  • 7. Prepare and submit (2–3 days) → Final checklist.

Keep moving

  • Write the Methods while you synthesise; they are already documented in your portfolios.
  • Book a co-author review before the final check, not after.
  • If the search will be more than a year old at submission, schedule an update.
