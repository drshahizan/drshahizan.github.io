SLR WRITING MODULES · W9

Limits and Risks of AI in SLRs

Where AI goes wrong in reviews, and how to guard against it.

Learning outcomes

  • Recognise the specific failure modes of AI in evidence synthesis
  • Design checks that catch AI errors
  • Report AI use so the review remains reproducible

Key points

Failure modes

  • Fabricated studies and references: chatbots can produce plausible but non-existent citations.
  • Missed records: AI search assistants do not search databases exhaustively and cannot replace a documented search.
  • Screening errors: prioritisation tools can miss relevant studies if screening stops too early.
  • Extraction errors: numbers and outcomes can be misread or invented.
  • Bias: tools may favour well-cited, English-language or recent papers.
  • Irreproducibility: outputs change between model versions and sessions.

Safeguards

  • Run and report searches in bibliographic databases.
  • Use a pre-specified stopping rule for AI-prioritised screening and report it.
  • Verify every AI-extracted value against the full text.
  • Check every reference with the reference checker.
  • Record tool versions, dates and prompts.

When not to use AI

  • Making final inclusion decisions.
  • Assigning quality ratings without human appraisal.
  • Writing conclusions or research gaps you have not derived from the evidence.
