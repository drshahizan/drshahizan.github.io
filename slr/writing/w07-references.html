SLR WRITING MODULES · W7

References and Citation Integrity in SLRs

Managing included studies and background references accurately.

Learning outcomes

  • Distinguish included studies from background references
  • Verify every included study and check for retractions
  • Present the list of included studies transparently

Key points

Two kinds of references

  • Included studies are your data. Each must be traceable from the PRISMA diagram to Table 1 to the reference list.
  • Background references support the Introduction and Discussion.
  • Mark included studies (for example with an asterisk or study IDs), or list them separately, as the journal allows.

Verification

  • Check DOI, authors, year and journal for every included study against the original record.
  • Check for retractions and expressions of concern; a retracted study should normally be excluded or its status discussed.
  • Never cite a study suggested by an AI tool without opening and reading it.

Reference management

  • Insert citations only through a reference manager and switch to the journal’s style at the end.
  • Keep the screening library (all records) separate from the manuscript library.
  • If the list of included studies is long, provide it as a supplementary file.

Example: A citation that cannot be traced

WeakAI tutors will replace teaching assistants within five years (Smith et al., 2025).
StrongerTwo of the 42 studies examined the replacement of teaching assistants, and neither found evidence for it [S12, S27].
