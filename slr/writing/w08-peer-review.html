SLR WRITING MODULES · W8

Peer Review and Responding for SLRs

Anticipating and answering what reviewers ask about systematic reviews.

Learning outcomes

  • Review your own SLR as a journal reviewer would
  • Write a point-by-point response letter
  • Handle disagreement and requests to update the search

Key points

Review yourself first

  • Run the “Simulate a journal reviewer of your SLR” prompt and the Final Pre-Submission Checklist before you submit.
  • Ask a colleague outside your team to read the Methods and try to reproduce one search.

What reviewers ask about SLRs

  • Reproducibility of the search and coverage of sources.
  • Single-reviewer screening and agreement statistics.
  • Missing or unused quality appraisal.
  • Justification for not doing a meta-analysis.
  • Contribution compared with existing reviews.
  • Consistency of counts.

Responding

  • The response letter template, principles and eleven model replies are in Responding to Reviewers of an SLR.
  • The review timeline, desk rejection and revision decisions are in After Submission.

Example: Responding to a comment

WeakWe have revised the paper accordingly.
StrongerWe added the full search strings for all three databases with the date last searched (Supplementary Table S2) and revised Section 3.2 (p. 6, lines 118–140).
