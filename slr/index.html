Questions

Before you submit

Who can submit?

Participants registered for Stage 1 and Stage 2 (Cohort 1 or Cohort 2). Use the same Matric / Staff No. and email you registered with.

Will every submission be accepted?

No. Places in Stage 3 and Stage 4 are by selection, based on the completeness and quality of the three documents. The secretariat will contact selected participants.

Can I change my submission?

Yes, before the deadline. Submit the form again with the same details; only your latest submission is reviewed. Do not edit your files after the deadline.

Which links are accepted?

Google Drive, Google Docs or Sheets, and OneDrive or SharePoint. Make sure anyone with the link can open and comment on each file.

Can I submit after the deadline?

No. The form closes automatically at 5:00 PM on 30 November 2026 (Malaysia time), and the time recorded by the server is final.
