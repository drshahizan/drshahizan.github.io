Before you come

  • A laptop with power adapter, and access to Scopus or Web of Science through the UTM library.
  • Your article draft, quality assessment Excel file and PRISMA diagram (the documents you submitted).
  • Zotero with your included studies in one collection.
  • Two or three journals you are considering, with their guide-for-authors links.
