Day 1 · Discover

Literature Discovery and Search Strategy

From a focused topic to a reproducible search and an organised initial article set.

21 October 2026
TimeSessionHands-on activity and tools
8:30–9:00Arrival and setupCheck each participant’s topic, laptop, institutional database access and SLR workspace. Confirm Zotero is installed or accessible.
9:00–9:451. Topic and scopeTurn a broad area into a manageable review topic. Define the population/context, central concept and scope limits; record a first scope statement.
9:45–10:302. Research questionsDraft answerable review questions, check alignment with the scope, and use Gemini to critique clarity and feasibility. Participants decide which suggestions to accept.
10:30–10:45Morning break—
10:45–11:303. Keyword matrixBuild concept groups, synonyms and spelling variants. Ask Gemini for candidate terms, verify them against seed papers and subject terminology, then complete the matrix.
11:30–12:30Build and test a Boolean stringCombine synonyms with OR and concepts with AND. Use the Stage 1 string builder and Gemini to draft alternatives, then inspect syntax and scope before testing.
12:30–1:30Lunch break—
1:30–2:154. Select search sourcesCompare Scopus and Web of Science as citation indexes with IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect and SpringerLink as specialist or publisher platforms. Select sources relevant to the topic and confirm access.
2:15–3:00Trial database searchRun a trial query in a selected database. Use Scopus AI to explore concepts and related literature, then verify and execute the formal search in the database itself.
3:00–3:15Afternoon break—
3:15–4:005. Execute and refineInspect a sample of titles and abstracts, adjust unclear or over-restrictive terms, and record the exact query, date, filters and results count in the Search Log.
4:00–4:306. Organise initial recordsExport relevant records to Zotero and organise them in a collection. Demonstrate Publish or Perish for supplementary citation discovery; record it separately from formal database searches.
4:30–5:007. Portfolio checkpointReview the Stage 1 portfolio and identify missing items before the participant continues searching.

Stage 1 participant output

Focused topic and scope, research questions, keyword matrix, draft Boolean search string, selected sources, search log and an initial organised article set.

Search integrity: Scopus AI and Gemini support exploration and drafting. The reproducible search must be run in the selected database or platform and recorded with its exact query, date, filters and result count. Publish or Perish is supplementary discovery, not a substitute for a documented database search.

Day 2 · Review

Systematic Review and Evidence Synthesis

Practise the review workflow on a sample, then apply the protocol consistently to the participant’s own records.

22 October 2026
TimeSessionHands-on activity and tools
8:30–9:00Stage 1 handoverCheck Stage 1 search exports, open the Stage 2 practice pack, and identify the sample records for practice. Keep the original database exports unchanged.
9:00–9:451. Confirm protocol and eligibilityReview the question, inclusion/exclusion criteria, publication types, date range and decision rules. Test criteria on sample records and clarify ambiguous cases.
9:45–10:302. Deduplicate recordsImport sample records into Zotero or Rayyan, identify duplicate records, retain a clean set and record the before/after counts.
10:30–10:45Morning break—
10:45–12:003. Screen titles and abstractsCalibrate decisions on a shared sample, then screen independently in Rayyan using include, exclude or uncertain. Apply protocol-linked exclusion reasons.
12:00–12:30Reconcile decisionsCompare disagreements, discuss uncertain records and note any protocol clarification needed before full screening.
12:30–1:30Lunch break—
1:30–2:154. Screen full textsRetrieve sample full texts or use the full-text simulation, apply the same criteria and record one clear primary reason for each exclusion. Use the review workbook to preserve decisions.
2:15–2:455. Reconcile PRISMA countsEnter sample counts at identification, deduplication, screening and full-text stages. Check that the flow is arithmetically consistent.
2:45–3:00Afternoon break—
3:00–3:456. Pilot quality assessmentSelect an appraisal checklist appropriate to the study design, assess sample papers and record evidence for each judgement.
3:45–4:307. Pilot data extractionTest the extraction matrix on sample papers. Use Gemini Notebook to locate relevant evidence in uploaded documents, request page or section references, and verify each entry against the source paper.
4:30–5:00Portfolio and independent work planReview the Stage 2 portfolio, assign next actions and set a target for completing the real review set before Stage 3.

Stage 2 training-day output

Confirmed protocol and eligibility rules, a deduplicated practice set, calibrated screening decisions, a sample PRISMA flow, and piloted quality-assessment and extraction forms.

Independent work · 23 October–17 November: Participants complete screening, full-text assessment, quality appraisal and data extraction for their own review. The final selected studies, PRISMA counts and extraction framework should be ready before Stage 3 on 18 November. Gemini Notebook supports document analysis; participants remain responsible for checking every finding in the original paper.
