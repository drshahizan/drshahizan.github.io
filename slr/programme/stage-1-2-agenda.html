Literature Discovery and Search Strategy

From a focused topic to a documented search and organised article set.

8:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m.
TimeSessionHands-on activity and tools
8:30–9:00Arrival and setupCheck topic, laptop and database access; open the SLR workspace and Zotero.
9:00–9:451. Topic and scopeNarrow a broad area, define its key concepts and boundaries, and record a scope statement.
9:45–10:302. Research questionsDraft answerable questions. Use Gemini to critique clarity and feasibility; review suggestions before adopting them.
10:30–10:45Morning break—
10:45–11:303. Keyword matrixGroup concepts and synonyms. Ask Gemini for candidate terms, then verify against seed papers and subject terminology.
11:30–12:30Build a Boolean search stringCombine synonyms with OR and concepts with AND. Use the string builder and Gemini to draft and test the query.
12:30–1:30Lunch break—
1:30–2:154. Select search sourcesCompare Scopus and Web of Science citation indexes with IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect and SpringerLink platforms; choose sources that fit the review.
2:15–3:00Trial database searchTest the query in a selected source. Use Scopus AI to explore related concepts, then run and verify the formal search in the database.
3:00–3:15Afternoon break—
3:15–4:005. Refine and log the searchInspect titles and abstracts; refine terms. Record the exact query, date, filters and result count in the Search Log.
4:00–4:306. Organise initial recordsExport records to Zotero. Demonstrate Publish or Perish for supplementary citation discovery.
4:30–5:007. Portfolio checkpointCheck the Stage 1 portfolio and list any missing items.
Participant output: Scope, research questions, keyword matrix, search string, selected sources, search log and initial article set.
Note: Gemini and Scopus AI support exploration. Record formal searches in the selected database; treat Publish or Perish as supplementary discovery.

Systematic Review and Evidence Synthesis

Practise the review workflow, then apply it to the participant’s own records.

8:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m.
TimeSessionHands-on activity and tools
8:30–9:00Stage 1 handoverCheck search exports and open the Stage 2 practice pack. Keep original database exports unchanged.
9:00–9:451. Confirm protocolReview the question, eligibility criteria and decision rules; test them on sample records.
9:45–10:302. Deduplicate recordsImport sample records to Zotero or Rayyan; identify duplicates and record before/after counts.
10:30–10:45Morning break—
10:45–12:003. Screen titles and abstractsCalibrate decisions on a shared sample, then screen in Rayyan as include, exclude or uncertain.
12:00–12:30Reconcile decisionsDiscuss disagreements and clarify protocol rules before full-text screening.
12:30–1:30Lunch break—
1:30–2:154. Screen full textsUse sample papers or the full-text simulation. Record one protocol-linked reason for each exclusion.
2:15–2:455. Reconcile PRISMA countsEnter sample counts for identification, deduplication, screening and full-text stages; check the totals.
2:45–3:00Afternoon break—
3:00–3:456. Pilot quality assessmentSelect an appraisal checklist suited to the study design and record evidence for sample judgements.
3:45–4:307. Pilot data extractionTest the extraction matrix. Use Gemini Notebook to locate evidence and page references, then verify entries in the source papers.
4:30–5:00Portfolio and work planCheck the Stage 2 portfolio and plan completion of the real review set.
Training-day output: Confirmed protocol, practice screening, sample PRISMA counts, and piloted quality-assessment and extraction forms.
Independent work · 23 October–17 November: Complete screening, full-text assessment, quality appraisal and extraction for the actual review before Stage 3 on 18 November. Verify Gemini Notebook findings against each paper.
