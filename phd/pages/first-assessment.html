First Assessment

Everything you need to prepare, complete and submit for your research proposal (First Assessment) at the Faculty of Computing.

WhenRequirementsFormsTurnitinSubmitSteps

When to submit

EarliestSemester 1

PhD students may submit the proposal for First Assessment from their first semester.

LatestSemester 3

Active students who have not submitted by the maximum semester receive an Unsatisfactory (TM) result for that semester.

FailSemester 4

Not submitting by the fourth semester means two consecutive TM results and a Fail (KG).

Requirements

Items marked Submit here must also be submitted on the Submit page for your supervisor’s verification.

  1. Research proposal (4 copies) Submit here
    • MUST be verified (signed & stamped) by the supervisor.
    • Presentation slides are also submitted here.
  2. Plagiarism Report Form (4 copies)
    • MUST be verified (signed & stamped) by the programme coordinator.
    • The similarity index for each chapter must not exceed 20%; the form also records the AI similarity for each chapter.
    • Email the form (PDF) directly to your programme coordinator (see Turnitin).
  3. Results of Research Methodology and the University General Course
    • You must have completed, be taking, or have credit transfer approval for both courses.
  4. Research Ethics Form Submit here
    • Complete Sections A and B and submit them together with the proposal.
  5. AI Usage Declaration Form Submit here
    • If you used any generative AI tool, complete every section and attach the prompt log as an appendix.
  6. Logbook Submit here
    • Signed by your supervisor, recording at least six progress meetings per semester.
    • Use the logbook template or GSMS. Remote students may have it signed by the main or co-supervisor at their location.
  7. NHP Checklist Submit here
    • Signed by you and your supervisor, and submitted to the Academic Office for Notis-Hantar-Proposal (NHP) approval. It requires:
    • at least one article submitted or published in PARS seminars or conference proceedings (attach the submission or acceptance evidence);
    • a presentation at a seminar or colloquium organised by a Faculty Research Group (attach the certificate, invitation letter or agenda);
    • the signed logbook (item 6) and Research Methodology / University General Course results (item 3);
    • two examiners nominated by your supervisor (three for fast-track students or UTM staff).

Step by step

  1. Prepare. Complete the proposal in the UTM thesis template, keep your logbook up to date and collect your PARS and seminar evidence.
  2. Check similarity. Run each chapter through Turnitin (at most three uploads), fill in the Plagiarism Report Form and email it to your programme coordinator for endorsement.
  3. Complete the forms. Research Ethics (Sections A and B), AI Usage Declaration and the NHP Checklist.
  4. Submit on PhD Hub for your supervisor’s verification. Your supervisor signs and stamps the proposal and nominates the examiners.
  5. Hand in to the Academic Office. The NHP Checklist with evidence, 4 copies of the proposal and 4 copies of the endorsed plagiarism report.
  6. Present. The panel completes Section C of the Research Ethics form during your presentation.
