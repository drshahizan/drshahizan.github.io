Sign in

Use your UTM graduate email and your matric number. First time without a registered email? Sign in with your @graduate.utm.my email and matric number to register it.

Set sharing to Anyone with the link · Viewer, then test the link in a private window. UTM OneDrive links are often limited to UTM accounts.

On the website, show

“Title only” is the default for a thesis under examination. Choose “Title and link” only if you are happy to share it publicly.

Supervisor only. Drafts are seen by your supervisor only and never appear on the website.

What you can submit

  • First Assessment: research proposal and slides
  • Draft for supervisor review: any chapter or draft, seen by your supervisor only
  • Thesis submitted for examination (before viva)
  • Viva slides
  • Final thesis (after viva corrections)
  • Articles: journal or conference, one entry per title
Submitting again
A new submission of the same document replaces the earlier one (articles and drafts with a different title are added). Your supervisor keeps the full history.

Keep your receipt (screenshot or print) as proof of submission.
