Prospective students

I welcome applications for PhD and MSc by research in data science, machine learning, ontology and semantic systems, learning analytics and information systems.

01

Before you write to me

  • Read a few recent papers from the group.
  • Look through current students’ topics to see where your idea fits.
02

Prepare

  • A 2–3 page concept note: problem, objectives, proposed method.
  • Your CV and academic transcripts.
03

Apply

  • Contact me with your concept note.
  • Apply through the UTM School of Graduate Studies portal, naming me as proposed supervisor.

Topics currently open

Any of the group’s research areas. New ideas in AI and data science are welcome.
