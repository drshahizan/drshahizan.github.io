PhD guide

The usual path through a PhD in the group, what to prepare at each stage, and where to find examples.

The daily life of a PhD student

The daily life of a PhD student may differ significantly from that of an undergraduate or Masters student. There will be much more independence and very few ‘taught’ elements. A typical week will almost certainly include the same number of study hours as a full-time job.

Stages and checklists

1

Proposal

Define the problem, objectives and scope, then defend the proposal before the panel.

  • Problem statement and research questions agreed with your supervisor
  • Draft proposal shared on the Submit page
  • Slides rehearsed before the defence
Example proposals →
2

Literature review

Build Chapter 2 systematically (SLR / PRISMA).

  • Search strategy and inclusion criteria written down
  • PRISMA flow and synthesis tables
  • Chapter 2 draft shared
Chapter 2 guide →
3

Methodology & experiments

Design the framework, collect data, run experiments and keep code and data versioned.

  • Research framework diagram
  • Data and code in a versioned repository
  • Chapter 3 draft shared
4

Results & publications

Turn each objective into a journal or conference paper before submitting the thesis.

  • One paper per objective, submitted or published
  • Chapters 4–5 drafts shared
  • Papers listed on the Submit page
Group publications →
5

Thesis & viva

Write up, submit and prepare the viva presentation.

  • Full thesis draft shared for review
  • Viva slides rehearsed with the group
  • Corrections tracked after the viva
