PhD guide

The usual path through a PhD in the group, what to prepare at each stage, and where to find examples.

The daily life of a PhD student

The daily life of a PhD student may differ significantly from that of an undergraduate or Masters student. There will be much more independence and very few ‘taught’ elements. A typical week will almost certainly include the same number of study hours as a full-time job.

Stages and checklists

1

Research proposal & First Assessment

Define the problem, objectives and scope, then present them at the First Assessment.

  • Problem statement and research questions agreed with your supervisor
  • Proposal submitted as Research Proposal · First Assessment
  • Slides rehearsed and submitted as Slides · First Assessment
Example proposals →   Example slides →
2

Research & writing

Review the literature, build the method, run experiments and write chapter by chapter.

  • Chapter 2 built systematically (SLR / PRISMA)
  • Data and code kept in a versioned repository
  • Each chapter sent as a Draft for supervisor review (seen by your supervisor only)
Chapter 2 guide and resources →
3

Articles

Turn each objective into a journal or conference article before submitting the thesis.

  • One article per objective, submitted or published
  • Each article added as Article · Journal / Conference
Students’ articles →   Group publications →
4

Thesis for examination & viva

Submit the thesis for examination, then prepare and rehearse the viva.

  • Thesis submitted as Thesis · Submitted for Examination (title only by default)
  • Viva slides rehearsed with the group and submitted as Slides · Viva
Past viva slides →
5

Final thesis

Complete the viva corrections and submit the final thesis.

  • Corrections tracked against the examiners’ report
  • Final version submitted as Thesis · Final (after viva)
