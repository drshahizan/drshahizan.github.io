FIVE TRAINING MODULES

From literature discovery to academic writing.

Each module below has its own companion page with a hands-on tool you can use during the session — a search string builder, prompt builders, a chart-type helper and quick references. See the full schedule for exact timings and breaks.

MODULE 1 · 9:00 – 10:15 AM

Literature Discovery and Understanding

Discover relevant scholarly literature, explore peer-reviewed research and understand key information from academic papers. Includes a Boolean Search String Builder and a hands-on Scopus AI exercise.

Explore Module 1 → MODULE 2 · 10:30 – 11:30 AM

Idea Generation and Research Design

Generate and refine research ideas, formulate research questions and develop an initial research design. Includes a Research Question & Design Prompt Builder and a FINER quick reference.

Explore Module 2 → MODULE 3 · 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM

Knowledge Organisation and Mind Mapping

Organise research information and transform research concepts into structured mind maps. Includes a Knowledge Organisation Prompt Builder and recommended mind-mapping tools.

Explore Module 3 → MODULE 4 · 2:00 – 3:15 PM

Data and Research Visualisation

Interpret research findings and produce diagrams, infographics, charts or data narratives. Includes a Chart-Type Decision Helper.

Explore Module 4 → MODULE 5 · 3:30 – 4:30 PM

Academic Writing and Deep Research Assistance

Synthesise research information and improve the clarity, coherence and organisation of academic writing. Includes a citation quick reference and a responsible AI writing checklist.
