TENTATIVE PROGRAMME · 24 AUGUST 2026

One focused day, from literature to writing.

Duration: One day · Time: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM · Level: Beginner to intermediate.

FULL SCHEDULE

Five modules, two breaks, one wrap-up.

8:30 – 9:00 AMRegistration and Training Preparation — participants register and prepare their laptops, research topics and relevant materials.
9:00 – 10:15 AMModule 1: Literature Discovery and Understanding — discover relevant scholarly literature, explore peer-reviewed research and understand key information from academic papers.
10:15 – 10:30 AMMorning Break
10:30 – 11:30 AMModule 2: Idea Generation and Research Design — generate and refine research ideas, explore potential research problems, formulate research questions and develop an initial research design.
11:30 AM – 12:30 PMModule 3: Knowledge Organisation and Mind Mapping — organise research information, synthesise source-based knowledge and transform research concepts into structured mind maps.
12:30 – 2:00 PMLunch Break
2:00 – 3:15 PMModule 4: Data and Research Visualisation — interpret research findings, select appropriate visual formats and produce diagrams, infographics, charts or data narratives.
3:15 – 3:30 PMAfternoon Break
3:30 – 4:30 PMModule 5: Academic Writing and Deep Research Assistance — synthesise research information, develop structured academic arguments and improve the clarity, coherence and organisation of academic writing.
4:30 – 5:00 PMPresentation, Discussion, Q&A and Course Wrap-up — present selected outputs, discuss responsible AI practices and develop a personalised AI-assisted research workflow.

TRAINING MODULES IN DETAIL

What each module covers.

M1

Literature Discovery and Understanding

AI-assisted discovery of scholarly literature, exploration of peer-reviewed research, paper understanding, preliminary synthesis and verification of original sources.

Idea Generation and Research Design

Development and refinement of research ideas, problems, questions, objectives and preliminary research designs through structured AI-assisted exploration.

Knowledge Organisation and Mind Mapping

Organisation of sources and research concepts into themes, literature matrices, knowledge structures and visual mind maps.

Data and Research Visualisation
