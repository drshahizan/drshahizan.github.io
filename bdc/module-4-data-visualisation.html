RECOMMENDED TOOLS

Tools for bibliometric mapping and scientific communication

Once you know the chart type you need, these tools help you build bibliometric network maps, topic overviews and polished scientific visuals.

01
BIBLIOMETRIC NETWORK

VOSviewer

Constructs and visualises co-authorship, citation and keyword co-occurrence networks.

02
BIBLIOMETRIC ANALYSIS

Bibliometrix

R-based toolkit for comprehensive bibliometric analysis and science mapping.

03
NETWORK ANALYSIS

Gephi

Open-source platform for visualising and analysing complex networks.

04
KNOWLEDGE MAP

Open Knowledge Maps

Open platform that creates visual overviews of research topics from scholarly literature.

05
SCIENTIFIC ILLUSTRATION

BioRender

Creates professional scientific illustrations for biology, medicine and the life sciences.

06
SCIENTIFIC FIGURES & COMMUNICATION

Canva

Creates presentations, posters, infographics and other visual research communication materials.
