FROM DISCOVERY TO SUBMISSION

Your end-to-end article workflow

Twelve stages from literature discovery and research design to analysis, submission and reviewer response. Each stage links to the most relevant module, prompt or checklist.

  1. 1
    MODULE 1 · LITERATURE DISCOVERY

    Discover and shortlist the literature

    Build a Boolean search string, search core databases and Scopus AI, then shortlist papers with citation snowballing and the CRAAP test.

    Go to Module 1 →
  2. 2
    MODULE 2 · IDEA GENERATION

    Generate and refine your research question

    Turn your shortlist into a problem tree, spot gaps across sources, and pressure-test candidate questions against the FINER criteria.

    Go to Module 2 →
  3. 3
    METHODOLOGY

    Design the methodology

    Align the research question with the design, sampling, data collection, validity requirements and planned analysis.

    Explore Methodology Prompts →
  4. 4
    MODULE 3 · KNOWLEDGE ORGANISATION

    Organise your evidence

    Build a research matrix and concept map so every source's method, findings and gap are structured before writing.

    Go to Module 3 →
  5. 5
    DATA PREPARATION

    Collect or prepare the data

    Document recruitment or dataset selection, instruments, procedures, preprocessing, ethics, deviations and the audit trail.

    Plan Data Collection →
  6. 6
    ANALYSIS & INTERPRETATION

    Analyse and interpret the results

    Check assumptions, apply the planned analysis and interpret only verified outputs without inventing or overstating findings.

    Explore Analysis Prompts →
  7. 7
    MODULE 4 · DATA VISUALISATION

    Visualise your findings

    Choose an appropriate chart and produce accurate, fact-checked figures, diagrams or infographics.

    Go to Module 4 →
  8. 8
    MODULE 5 · ACADEMIC WRITING

    Draft the manuscript

    Develop each section with AI as a responsible writing partner, verify every claim and prepare the AI disclosure statement.

    Go to Module 5 →
  9. 9
    SELF-REVIEW

    Complete the pre-submission checklist

    Check content, citations, AI use, formatting and technical details before anyone else reads the draft.

    Go to the Checklist ↓
  10. 10
    MANUSCRIPT REVIEW

    Conduct an evidence-grounded review

    Use an editorial review prompt to identify desk-rejection risks, major weaknesses and prioritised corrective actions.

    Explore Review Prompts →
  11. 11
    FORMAT, PACKAGE & SUBMIT

    Prepare the submission package

    Check similarity, references, journal formatting, cover letter, declarations and supplementary files before submission.

    Open Publication Prompts →
  12. 12
    REVISE & RESUBMIT

    Respond to reviewers

    Track each comment, revise the manuscript and prepare an evidence-based response with exact manuscript locations.

    Explore Reviewer-Response Workflows →
