FROM DISCOVERY TO SUBMISSION

Your end-to-end article workflow

Ten stages from your first literature search to a submitted, reviewer-ready manuscript. Each stage links to the module, tool or checklist you need at that point.

  1. 1
    MODULE 1 · LITERATURE DISCOVERY

    Discover and shortlist the literature

    Build a Boolean search string, search core databases and Scopus AI, then shortlist papers with citation snowballing and the CRAAP test.

    Go to Module 1 →
  2. 2
    MODULE 2 · IDEA GENERATION

    Generate and refine your research question

    Turn your shortlist into a problem tree, spot gaps across sources, and pressure-test candidate questions against the FINER criteria.

    Go to Module 2 →
  3. 3
    MODULE 3 · KNOWLEDGE ORGANISATION

    Organise your evidence

    Build a research matrix and concept map so every source's method, findings and gap are structured before you start writing.

    Go to Module 3 →
  4. 4
    MODULE 4 · DATA VISUALISATION

    Visualise your findings

    Choose the right chart type for your data and produce honest, fact-checked figures and infographics for the manuscript.

    Go to Module 4 →
  5. 5
    MODULE 5 · ACADEMIC WRITING

    Draft the manuscript

    Draft each section with AI as a responsible writing partner, verify every AI-assisted claim, and prepare your AI disclosure statement.

    Go to Module 5 →
  6. 6
    SELF-REVIEW

    Work through the pre-submission checklist

    Check content, citations, AI use, formatting and technical details yourself before anyone else reads the draft.

    Go to the Checklist ↓
  7. 7
    PEER / SUPERVISOR REVIEW

    Get an evidence-grounded review

    Use a manuscript review prompt or the SLR review builder so a supervisor, co-author or AI reviewer critiques the draft against a clear rubric.

    Explore Review Prompts →
  8. 8
    INTEGRITY CHECK

    Run a similarity and AI-writing check

    Submit the near-final draft to Turnitin (or your institution's checker) and review the report before it goes to co-authors.

    See Writing Tools →
  9. 9
    FORMAT & PACKAGE

    Format and package for submission

    Convert references to the target journal's citation style, finalise the cover letter, and confirm every author declaration is complete.

    Convert Citations →
  10. 10
    SUBMIT & RESPOND

    Submit and respond to reviewers

    Submit through the journal system, then track every reviewer comment with an evidence-based, manuscript-located response.

    Explore Agentic AI Workflows →
