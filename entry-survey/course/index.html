About SCST1133

Bachelor in Computer Science (Data Engineering) with Honours · 3 credit hours · Semester 1, 2026/2027

Course synopsis

This course introduces information technology and data engineering through core concepts, processes, tools and professional roles. Students examine data structures, file formats, data sources, programming languages, modern platforms, ETL/ELT, data pipelines, lifecycle management, security, governance, ethics and career pathways.

Learning combines lectures, active learning, hands-on activities, industry talks, an industry visit and authentic work-based learning.

Course Learning Outcomes

CLO1Explain basic concepts of information technology and their role in supporting data-driven systems through systems thinking.
CLO2Construct a basic data engineering lifecycle by applying appropriate technologies, best practices and industry standards.
CLO3Develop a prototype that addresses the data engineering ecosystem by applying the data engineering lifecycle while demonstrating ethical principles and professional responsibilities.
