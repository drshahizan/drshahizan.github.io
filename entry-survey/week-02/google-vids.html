Step 08 · Explain and present

Google Vids

Communicate your digital workflow through a focused 60–90 second technical video.

GVVideo
Technical communication

Tell a clear story

Google Vids supports video creation, editing, collaboration and sharing within Google Workspace.

Sign in and create a video

Open Google Vids

Visit vids.google.com and sign in with an eligible Google Account.

Check access

Use your UTM account if Google Vids is enabled. Creation features may depend on the institution’s Workspace settings.

Start your project

Create a blank video or use an available template. Select a landscape layout for this activity.

Video brief: My Digital Tools Journey

SceneSuggested content
1 · IntroductionYour name, course and activity objective.
2 · GitHubRepository, README and published GitHub Pages website.
3 · FigmaDashboard design and key design decision.
4 · Google StitchPrompt, generated interface and comparison.
5 · ReflectionWhat you learned and how the tools connect.
6 · ConclusionFinal outcome and project link.

Production checklist

  • Keep the duration between 60 and 90 seconds.
  • Use short on-screen text and readable visuals.
  • Write a concise script before recording narration.
  • Add captions where available.
  • Use consistent colours, spacing and transitions.
  • Check audio clarity and spelling before sharing.

Share and document

Set sharing permission

Select Share, choose an appropriate viewer permission and copy the link.

Test access

Open the URL in a private browser window. If required, export or download the finished video using the options available to your account.

Add it to README
