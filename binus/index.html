ABOUT THE SESSION

Research smarter without compromising scholarly rigour.

This practical guest lecture helps postgraduate students and researchers use artificial intelligence more efficiently, systematically and confidently. Participants explore Scopus AI, NotebookLM and other suitable tools for discovering literature, understanding papers, identifying research gaps, organising evidence and synthesising findings.

The session also introduces an effortless AI-assisted literature review workflow while emphasising source verification, academic integrity and human scholarly judgement.

Human-led research

AI accelerates research tasks. Final interpretation, verification and scholarly decisions remain the researcher’s responsibility.
