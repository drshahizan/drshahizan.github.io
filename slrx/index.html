Before you start

Come prepared to build your own SLR

Research directionBring a research topic or problem area you want to investigate.
Seed literatureBring several relevant papers that can anchor discovery and keyword development.
Database accessPrepare access to Scopus, Web of Science or other databases available to you.
Research workspaceBring your laptop and a reference-management workflow for hands-on activities.

Learning journey

Methodology first. Tools where they add value.
