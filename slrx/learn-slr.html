Quick distinction

Database, publisher, or index?

Bibliographic index

Web of Science and Scopus index publication records and citations.

Publisher platform

ScienceDirect and SpringerLink provide access to published content.

Journal metric source

JCR and SJR provide metrics reported with a year and subject category.
