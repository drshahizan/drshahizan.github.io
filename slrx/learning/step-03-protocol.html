Step 03 of 10

Develop the Review Protocol

Decide the method before seeing the results so that the review remains transparent and defensible.

What the protocol controls

The protocol is the blueprint for the review. It reduces ad hoc decisions and provides the foundation for the methodology section.

Specify in advance

  1. Background, purpose, objectives, and research questions.
  2. Databases, search fields, years, languages, and publication types.
  3. Inclusion and exclusion criteria.
  4. Deduplication and screening procedures.
  5. Quality assessment instrument and threshold.
  6. Data extraction fields and synthesis method.
  7. Reviewer roles, conflict resolution, and record keeping.
Protocol rule

If the protocol changes, record what changed, when it changed, and why. Do not silently modify criteria after screening begins.

Quality check

  • Another reviewer could repeat the planned process.
  • Criteria are operational and unambiguous.
  • Every RQ is linked to extraction and synthesis.
  • Roles and disagreement procedures are defined.

Required output

Approved SLR Protocol

  • Review objectives
  • Complete method
  • Decision rules
  • Planned evidence tables
  • Version and approval date
