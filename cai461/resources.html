Course textbooks

  • Wooldridge, M. (2009). An Introduction to MultiAgent Systems, 2nd ed. Wiley.BookCh. 2 for utility over runs and task specifications; Ch. 8 and 11 and Part III for cooperation and protocol properties. The backbone of Modules 1 and 2.
  • Russell, S. & Norvig, P. (2020). Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach, 4th ed. Pearson.BookCh. 2 §2.2 on rationality and performance measures; Ch. 18 on multiagent decision making.Book site
  • Shoham, Y. & Leyton-Brown, K. (2008). Multiagent Systems: Algorithmic, Game-Theoretic, and Logical Foundations. Cambridge University Press.BookCh. 3 on game theory and §6.4 on selfish routing and the price of anarchy. The authors offer the book as a free PDF.Free PDF
  • Wilensky, U. & Rand, W. (2015). An Introduction to Agent-Based Modeling. MIT Press.BookCh. 6 on analysing models and BehaviorSpace; Ch. 7 on verification, validation and replication.
  • Bellifemine, F., Caire, G. & Greenwood, D. (2007). Developing Multi-Agent Systems with JADE. Wiley.BookThe chapter on JADE tools covers the Sniffer, Introspector, RMA and Log Manager agents.JADE

Classic papers and standards

  • Smith, R. G. (1980). The Contract Net Protocol. IEEE Transactions on Computers, C-29(12), 1104–1113.PaperThe original Contract Net paper behind the message-count example.
  • Koutsoupias, E. & Papadimitriou, C. (1999). Worst-case equilibria. STACS 1999.PaperIntroduces the idea later named the price of anarchy.
  • FIPA (2002). ACL Message Structure Specification, SC00061G.StandardDefines conversation-id, reply-with, in-reply-to and protocol, the correlation fields for tracing.Specification
  • Grimm, V. et al. (2020). The ODD protocol for describing agent-based and other simulation models: a second update. JASSS 23(2), 7.PaperThe current version of ODD, with guidance for every element.Read online
  • Wilensky, U. & Rand, W. (2007). Making models match: replicating an agent-based model. JASSS 10(4), 2.PaperA worked replication study and practical advice on docking two implementations.Read online

LLM agent benchmarks

  • Liu, X. et al. (2024). AgentBench: Evaluating LLMs as Agents. ICLR 2024.BenchmarkEight environments, from operating systems to games, with task-specific success scores.PaperCode
  • Mialon, G. et al. (2024). GAIA: A Benchmark for General AI Assistants. ICLR 2024.Benchmark466 real-world questions that need tools and browsing, scored by exact final answer.PaperDataset
  • Jimenez, C. E. et al. (2024). SWE-bench: Can Language Models Resolve Real-World GitHub Issues? ICLR 2024.Benchmark2,294 real issues; a patch counts only if the project’s tests pass.PaperLeaderboard
  • Yao, S., Shinn, N., Razavi, P. & Narasimhan, K. (2025). τ-bench: A Benchmark for Tool-Agent-User Interaction in Real-World Domains. ICLR 2025.BenchmarkIntroduces pass^k. The repository now points to τ²-bench, which adds a telecom domain and fixes.Paperτ-bench codeτ²-bench code
  • Zhu, K. et al. (2025). MultiAgentBench: Evaluating the Collaboration and Competition of LLM Agents. ACL 2025.BenchmarkStar, chain, tree and graph topologies with milestone KPIs and coordination scores.PaperMARBLE code
