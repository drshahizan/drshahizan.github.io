Modules

Six modules that follow the lecture. Each one climbs a layer of the evaluation framework, so Module 6 can put them back together.

Before Module 1, spend five minutes on the big picture to see how the six modules connect.

  • 1What does “performing well” mean?Performance measures, rationality, expected utility, achievement and maintenance tasks.15 min
  • 2Evaluating a society of agentsSocial welfare, Pareto efficiency, Nash stability, price of anarchy and protocol criteria.18 min
  • 3Measuring the systemSix metric families, scalability and robustness curves, tracing, JADE tools and sound experiments.15 min
  • 4Evaluating agent-based modelsVerification, validation, replication, sensitivity analysis and the ODD protocol.12 min
  • 5Evaluating LLM-based agentsBenchmarks, pass^k reliability, cost, MultiAgentBench, MAST, LLM-as-a-judge and tracing.22 min
  • 6Bringing it togetherThe four-layer evaluation framework, the case activity and key takeaways.22 min

How the modules map to the four layers

The framework from Module 6 is the map for the whole session. Each layer adds questions that the layer below cannot answer.

LayerQuestion it answersModule
4 · Trust and deploymentWill it work every time, at an acceptable cost, without breaking rules?Module 5
3 · SystemIs it fast, does it scale, does it survive failures?Module 3, Module 4
2 · InteractionIs the group outcome good and stable?Module 2
1 · Individual agentDoes each agent pursue the right goal?Module 1

Self-check quizzes

Every module ends with a short multiple-choice quiz that marks itself and explains each answer. Nothing is recorded; they are for your own revision.
