Interactive tools

Six small calculators for the worked examples in the lecture. Change the numbers, watch the verdict change, and use them to check your own answers in the case activity.

Tools

  2. Two-agent game explorer

  5. Contract Net messages
  6. Accuracy against cost
Module 1

Expected utility calculator

Two agents, one success probability and two utilities each. The lecture’s example is pre-filled: A wins 7.0 to 6.3. Try giving failure a negative utility and see when B takes over.

Agent B

A succeeds70%

EU = P(success)·u(success) + (1 − P(success))·u(failure)

Try: u(failure) = −10 for both. A drops to 4.0 and B rises above it at 5.3, because B fails less often.

Price of anarchy in Pigou’s network

Module 5

pass@k and pass^k

The dashed grey line is pass@k (at least one success). The teal line is pass^k (every trial succeeds) when trials are independent. Add a share of tasks the agent never solves to see the amber line: the same average success rate, but failures concentrated on the same tasks.

Try p = 61% and 30% never-solved tasks. pass^8 lands near 25%, much closer to τ-bench’s reported GPT-4o result than the independent formula’s 2%.
